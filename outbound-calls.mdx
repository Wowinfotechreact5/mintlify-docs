Outbound calling allows a Valuez AI agent to initiate telephone conversations with approved contacts.

Valuez AI provides two outbound calling experiences:

| Outbound option | Purpose | Best used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Start Test Call** | Places one immediate call to a specified telephone number | Testing an agent over a real telephone connection |
| **Call Campaigns** | Calls a selected list of contacts according to campaign settings | Sales, follow-up, qualification, outreach, and other approved business campaigns |

Use **Start Test Call** while preparing an agent. Use **Call Campaigns** when the agent has been tested and you are ready to contact a managed list of people.

<Warning>
  Place outbound calls only to people your organization is legally permitted to contact. Follow all applicable consent, privacy, telemarketing, calling-hour, recording, and opt-out requirements.
</Warning>

---

## Common Uses

Outbound AI calls can support approved workflows such as:

- Following up with interested customers
- Qualifying sales leads
- Contacting customers about products or services
- Conducting approved customer surveys
- Sharing appointment-related information
- Following up on enquiries
- Re-engaging existing customers
- Running structured information campaigns
- Collecting approved customer responses
- Routing qualified opportunities to the appropriate team

The agent should not make guarantees, promises, offers, or commitments that have not been approved by your organization.

- Accurate product and service information
- Appropriate conversation settings
- A configured voice

## Quick Outbound Test Call

A quick outbound test places one call from a selected AI agent to an authorized customer or tester number.

This is different from launching a campaign. It is intended for controlled testing.

### Required Information

| Field | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Select Agent** | Yes | The agent that will handle the conversation |
| **Customer Number** | Yes | The telephone number that will receive the test call |
| **Virtual Number** | Yes | A purchased number belonging to your workspace |

Only virtual numbers owned by the current workspace are displayed.

---

## Start a Single Test Call

You can open the outbound test through **Virtual Phone Integration** or use **Voice Test** from the agent-testing workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the agent">
    Open **AI Voice Agents** and select the agent you want to test.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Voice Test or Add Integration">
    From **Test Agent**, select **Voice Test**. Alternatively, open **Add Integration** and choose the **Outgoing Calls** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the agent">
    Make sure the correct agent is selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the customer number">
    Enter a telephone number you own or are authorized to call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the virtual number">
    Choose a purchased virtual phone number from the workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Start Test Call">
    Wait while Valuez AI initiates the outbound call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the call">
    Accept the incoming call on the test telephone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the test">
    Use a prepared script to evaluate the greeting, knowledge, voice, and ending.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When the request is accepted, Valuez AI displays a confirmation that the outbound call was triggered.

The result may include a call identifier and current call status.

<Warning>
  **Start Test Call** places a real call and can consume credits. Do not use a person’s telephone number without authorization.
</Warning>

## Test Call Checklist

During a single outbound test, confirm that:

- [ ] The correct telephone rings
- [ ] The expected virtual number is used
- [ ] The correct agent speaks
- [ ] The greeting identifies the business appropriately
- [ ] The purpose of the call is clear
- [ ] Two-way audio works
- [ ] Speech is recognized correctly
- [ ] The voice is understandable over the telephone
- [ ] The agent handles interruptions
- [ ] The agent answers approved questions correctly
- [ ] The agent does not invent information
- [ ] The ending is professional
- [ ] The call appears in the appropriate history area

## Outbound Call Campaigns

Use **Call Campaigns** when you need to contact multiple approved contacts.

A campaign combines:

- An AI voice agent
- Products or services to discuss
- A caller number
- Starting language
- Time zone and calling hours
- Call-volume limits
- Retry settings
- A contact list
- A scheduled launch time

The campaign setup uses four main steps:

1. Campaign Details
2. Call Settings
3. Add Contacts
4. Schedule & Review

---

## Open Call Campaigns

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Call Campaigns">
    From the dashboard navigation, open **Call Campaigns**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review existing campaigns">
    Check current campaigns before creating another campaign for the same purpose or contact list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Create Campaign">
    Open the campaign-creation workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete each step">
    Enter the campaign details, configure calls, add contacts, and review the schedule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or launch">
    Save the campaign as a draft if it is not ready, or launch it after completing all required checks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Step 1: Campaign Details

The first step defines the campaign’s identity, purpose, agent, and offering.

### Campaign Name

Enter a clear name that helps your team identify the campaign.

Good examples:

```text
October Product Follow-Up
Enterprise Trial Qualification
Mumbai Customer Renewal Campaign
English Service Enquiry Follow-Up
```

Avoid vague names such as:

```text
Test
Campaign 1
New Calls
Customers
```

A useful campaign name normally includes the purpose, audience, region, language, or time period.

---

### AI Voice Agent

Select the agent that will speak with campaign contacts.

The agent is required.

Before continuing, confirm that the selected agent:

- Is configured for the campaign language
- Has the correct knowledge base
- Understands the campaign purpose
- Has approved instructions
- Has passed browser testing
- Has passed a telephone test

<Warning>
  Changing the selected agent can materially change the campaign conversation. Repeat testing whenever the agent changes.
</Warning>

---

### Products to Offer

Select the products relevant to the campaign.

When multiple products are selected, arrange them in the intended priority order.

The selected products should have:

- Accurate names
- Current descriptions
- Approved prices
- Eligibility details
- Important limitations
- Clear next steps

Do not select unrelated products merely to increase the amount of information available to the agent.

---

### Services to Offer

Select the services relevant to the campaign.

If several services are selected, place the most important service first.

Verify that each service contains:

- Scope
- Price or price range
- Eligibility
- Availability
- Location limitations
- Exclusions
- Booking or follow-up instructions

---

### Campaign Purpose

Describe why the campaign is being created.

A useful purpose helps your team understand the intended conversation.

Example:

```text
Follow up with customers who requested information about the Growth Plan,
answer approved questions, identify interest, and explain the next available
step without making unapproved pricing or service commitments.
```

The campaign purpose should clearly state:

- Who will be contacted
- Why they are being contacted
- What the agent should discuss
- What outcome the campaign is intended to produce

---

## Step 2: Call Settings

Call Settings control how and when calls are placed.

### Caller Number

Select the virtual phone number used for campaign calls.

The Caller Number is required.

Only purchased numbers available to the workspace are shown.

Before selecting a number, confirm that:

- The number is active
- It belongs to the correct workspace
- It is approved for outbound use
- It is not scheduled for deactivation
- Your team recognizes its business purpose

---

### Starting Language

Select the language the agent should use when the call begins.

The selected language must be enabled for the chosen agent.

If a language does not appear as available for the agent, review the agent’s language and voice configuration before continuing.

<Warning>
  Do not select a language only because it appears in the campaign form. Confirm that the agent, speech recognition, voice, and knowledge have been tested in that language.
</Warning>

---

### Time Zone

Select the time zone used to interpret the campaign’s calling hours and schedule.

This is especially important when contacts, campaign operators, or business teams are in different locations.

Before launching, confirm that:

- The time zone matches the intended audience
- The scheduled time is correct in that time zone
- Calls comply with applicable calling-hour restrictions
- Daylight-saving or regional time differences have been considered where relevant

---

### Allowed Calling Hours

Set the start and end time during which campaign calls may be placed.

The end time must be at least one hour after the selected start time.

Example:

| Setting | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Start time | 10:00 |
| End time | 18:00 |

Choose a time window that is:

- Appropriate for the audience
- Consistent with local regulations
- Suitable for the campaign purpose
- Supported by your operating team

Do not use broad calling windows merely to finish a campaign faster.

---

### Maximum Concurrent Calls

Maximum Concurrent Calls controls how many campaign calls can run simultaneously.

The allowed maximum depends on your subscription plan.

The interface displays the current plan limit beside the field.

Examples of the configured plan limits are:

| Plan | Maximum concurrent calls |
| --- | --: |
| **Basic or Free** | Campaign access unavailable |
| **Silver** | Up to 5 |
| **Gold** | Up to 10 |
| **Platinum** | Up to 15 |

Your workspace may receive limit information directly from its active plan. Use the limit displayed in the dashboard as the authoritative value.

Enter a whole number from `1` to your displayed plan limit.

### Choosing a Concurrency Level

A higher value can process contacts faster, but it can also:

- Use credits more quickly
- Produce more simultaneous customer conversations
- Increase the number of results your team must review
- Increase the number of potential human follow-ups

Start with a controlled concurrency level and increase it only after reviewing campaign quality.

---

### Daily Call Limit

Daily Call Limit controls how many campaign calls should be attempted in one day.

Use this setting to:

- Control campaign pace
- Manage credits
- Match team capacity
- Avoid contacting too many people at once
- Spread a campaign over several days

Choose a limit based on your audience, plan, budget, and operational capacity.

---

### Retry Unanswered Calls

Enable **Retry unanswered calls** if Valuez AI should make another attempt when a contact does not answer.

When enabled, configure:

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Maximum Retries** | Number of additional attempts allowed |
| **Retry Interval (Hours)** | Time Valuez AI should wait between attempts |

Example:

```text
Maximum Retries: 1
Retry Interval: 4 hours
```

Use conservative retry settings. Repeated attempts may create a poor customer experience or conflict with your organization’s calling policy.

<Warning>
  A retry should not be treated as permission to repeatedly contact someone who has declined, opted out, or requested no further calls.
</Warning>

---

## Step 3: Add Contacts

A campaign must contain contacts before it can be launched.

Valuez AI supports two contact-entry methods:

- **Upload File**
- **Add Manually**

You can review, select, edit, or remove contacts before launch.

---

## Upload Contacts from a File

Use **Upload File** to add multiple campaign contacts from a CSV or Excel file.

You can drag the file into the upload area or browse for it.

### Required and Optional Data

The file must contain a telephone number column.

Recognized contact information includes:

| Field | Requirement | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ContactNumber** or **Mobile Number** | Required | Primary telephone number |
| **CustomerName** or **Name** | Recommended | Personalizes the conversation and reporting |
| **AlternateContactNumber** | Optional | Additional contact number |
| **Email** | Optional | Customer email information |
| **Address** | Optional | Address information |
| **City** | Optional | City |
| **District** | Optional | District |
| **State** | Optional | State |
| **Country** | Optional | Country |
| **Pincode** | Optional | Postal code |

A downloadable template is available from the campaign workflow.

The template uses the following column headings:

```csv
CustomerName,ContactNumber,AlternateContactNumber,Email,Address,City,District,State,Country,Pincode
```

<Tip>
  Download the current template from Valuez AI before preparing a large import. This reduces column-name and formatting errors.
</Tip>

---

### Contact Limits

The number of contacts allowed in a campaign file depends on the active plan.

Configured plan limits include:

| Plan | Contacts per campaign file |
| --- | --: |
| **Basic or Free** | Campaign access unavailable |
| **Silver** | Up to 50 |
| **Gold** | Up to 200 |
| **Platinum** | Up to 500 |

Use the limit displayed in the dashboard because workspace-specific plan data may override the standard values.

---

### Prepare the Contact File

Before uploading:

- Remove contacts you are not authorized to call
- Remove test or placeholder numbers
- Check telephone numbers for typing errors
- Remove duplicate contacts
- Use consistent column names
- Keep one person per row
- Confirm names are matched with the correct numbers
- Remove unnecessary personal information
- Confirm the file does not exceed the plan limit

<Warning>
  Do not upload contact lists purchased from an unverified source or lists that your organization is not legally permitted to use.
</Warning>

---

## Review Imported Contacts

After contacts are added, review the contacts table.

The table can show:

- Contact selection
- Customer name
- Phone number
- Contact status
- Available actions

You can:

- Search by name
- Select or deselect contacts
- Select all displayed contacts
- Edit a contact
- Delete a contact
- Remove an uploaded file and its associated contacts

Only the contacts selected for the campaign should be included when the campaign is saved or launched.

### Remove an Uploaded File

Removing an uploaded document also removes the contacts associated with that import.

Review the confirmation carefully before proceeding.

### Delete an Individual Contact

Deleting a contact removes it from the campaign.

Use this when:

- The number is incorrect
- The contact did not consent
- The contact is a duplicate
- The contact requested not to be called
- The person is outside the intended audience

## Save as Draft

Use **Save as Draft** when the campaign is not ready to launch.

## Campaign Actions

Depending on the campaign state and your permissions, you may be able to:

- Open campaign details
- Edit the campaign
- Reschedule it
- Pause it
- Resume or restart it
- Review contacts
- Review calls
- Review leads
- Review campaign performance

### Pause a Campaign

Pause a campaign when you need to stop new campaign activity temporarily.

Common reasons include:

- Incorrect information was discovered
- The agent is giving unsuitable answers
- The contact list requires review
- Credits are running low
- The business offer has changed
- A compliance issue has been reported

Pausing does not correct the underlying problem. Make the required change, retest the agent, and review the campaign before resuming.

### Resume or Restart

Resume or restart only after confirming:

- The campaign problem has been resolved
- The agent was retested
- Contact selection is correct
- The schedule remains appropriate
- Credits are sufficient

### Reschedule

Use rescheduling when the campaign start needs to change.

Confirm the new date, time, time zone, and calling hours before saving.

## Troubleshooting

### Call Campaigns Are Locked

Your current plan may not include outbound campaigns.

Review the plan information displayed in the dashboard and upgrade if required.

---

### No Caller Number Is Available

Possible causes:

- No virtual number has been purchased
- The number was deactivated
- The current workspace does not own a number
- The plan does not permit a virtual number
- The number list could not be loaded

Open **Virtual Phone Integration** and review the workspace’s purchased numbers.

---

### The Starting Language Cannot Be Selected

The campaign language must be enabled for the selected agent.

Review:

1. The selected agent
2. The agent language
3. Voice configuration
4. Speech-to-text configuration
5. Available language options

Save the agent and reopen the campaign if necessary.

---

### Maximum Concurrent Calls Is Rejected

Enter a whole number from `1` to the plan limit displayed beside the field.

If the plan limit is `0`, campaign access is not currently available.

---

### Contact File Will Not Upload

Check that:

- The file is CSV or Excel
- A telephone-number column exists
- The header uses a recognized name
- The number of contacts is within the plan limit
- The file is not corrupted
- Each row is structured consistently

Download a new contact template and move your approved data into it.

---

### Contacts Are Required

A campaign cannot be launched without contacts.

Upload a file or add contacts manually, then confirm that the intended contacts are selected.

---

### A Contact Number Is Rejected

For manual contact entry, the current form expects a valid 10-digit primary number.

Remove spaces, letters, and unsupported symbols before trying again.

---

### Start Date and Time Are Required

Select a valid future start date and start time in the final campaign step.

Also confirm the selected time zone.

---

### The Campaign Does Not Start

Check:

- Campaign status
- Start date and time
- Time zone
- Calling hours
- Available credits
- Caller number
- Selected agent
- Contact selection
- Plan access
- Concurrency setting

If the campaign remains in an unexpected state, record the campaign name, scheduled time, and displayed status before contacting support.

---

### Calls Are Not Being Answered

This does not always indicate a platform problem.

Check:

- Contact-number accuracy
- Caller-number reputation
- Calling time
- Audience expectations
- Retry settings
- Call status in Campaign Call History

Avoid repeatedly increasing retries without reviewing customer impact.

---

### Calls Fail Immediately

Possible causes include:

- Invalid contact numbers
- Inactive virtual number
- Insufficient credits
- Calling restrictions
- Telephone-provider rejection
- Incomplete agent configuration

Review the individual call status and available conversation details.

---

### The Agent Gives Incorrect Information

Determine whether the problem comes from:

| Problem | Where to review it |
| --- | --- |
| Incorrect product information | Products |
| Incorrect service information | Services |
| Missing common answer | FAQs |
| Outdated policy | Knowledge Documents |
| Wrong campaign behavior | Agent Instructions |
| Wrong question recognized | Speech to Text |
| Unclear pronunciation | Voice & Audio |
| Wrong agent selected | Campaign Details |

Pause the campaign if incorrect information could affect customers.

Correct the issue, test the agent, and review the campaign before resuming.

---

### Changes Do Not Affect Active Calls

Changes may not affect conversations that have already started.

After changing the agent or campaign:

1. Save the change.
2. Test the agent.
3. Confirm the campaign settings.
4. Use a new controlled call.
5. Review the new call record.

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Start with a small campaign">
    Use a limited approved contact group first. Review the results before increasing call volume.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Use one clear campaign purpose">
    An agent performs more consistently when the campaign has a specific audience, offer, and expected outcome.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Keep the opening concise">
    Introduce the organization and purpose quickly. Long introductions increase the chance that contacts will disconnect.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Use conservative retry settings">
    Retry only when appropriate. Do not retry contacts who decline, opt out, or request no further communication.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Match concurrency to team capacity">
    If successful calls create follow-up work, ensure your team can handle the resulting volume.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Review early campaign calls">
    Inspect initial conversations before allowing a large scheduled campaign to continue.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Pause when information is wrong">
    Do not allow an agent to continue making calls while it is giving incorrect prices, policies, or commitments.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Maintain a suppression process">
    Remove contacts who opt out and ensure they are not reintroduced through a future upload.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Monitor credits">
    Campaigns can use credits quickly, especially with concurrent calls and retries.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Retest after significant changes">
    Changes to knowledge, instructions, voice, language, or products should be tested before resuming campaign activity.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
