Team Overview gives managers a consolidated view of sales-team calling activity in the Valuez AI workspace.

Use this page to monitor team size, assigned contacts, completed calls, conversion performance, recent call trends, workspace call outcomes, and the performance of individual team members.

<Info>
  Team Overview is designed for managers and authorized workspace users. The information visible to you may depend on your workspace permissions and the availability of sales-team data.
</Info>

---

## Open Team Overview

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to Valuez AI">
    Sign in to the workspace containing your sales team and assigned contacts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Sales Team">
    Select **Sales Team** from the main Valuez AI navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Team Overview">
    Click **Team Overview** to open the team-performance dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You Can Review

The Team Overview page helps you:

- See the total number of team members
- Review the total number of calls made
- Check how many calls were completed
- Understand the average number of calls per member
- Monitor the overall conversion rate
- Compare assigned and completed activity by member
- Review workspace-wide call outcomes
- Monitor call volume during the last seven days
- Identify top-performing team members
- Open a team member's detailed customer and call activity
- Navigate to the complete Team Members list

---

## Understanding the Dashboard

Team Overview is organized into four main areas:

| Area | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Date range** | Select the reporting period used by supported overview information |
| **Summary metrics** | View key team totals and performance indicators |
| **Performance charts** | Compare member activity, outcomes, and recent call volume |
| **Top Performers** | Review leading team members and open their detailed records |

The information updates using the latest data available from the workspace.

<Note>
  Some dashboard visualizations use fixed reporting periods. In particular, the member target comparison represents current daily activity, and the call-trend chart always displays the last seven days.
</Note>

---

## Select a Date Range

Use the date-range control near the top of the page to review supported team information for a selected period.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the date selector">
    Click the date-range control at the top of Team Overview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the reporting period">
    Select the start and end dates you want to review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the range">
    Apply the selection to refresh the supported overview information.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use **Reset** to return to the default date range.

<Info>
  The date range can affect the overview totals and top-performer information returned for the selected period. Charts that explicitly represent today or the last seven days continue to use their stated fixed periods.
</Info>

### Recommended date ranges

| Review Purpose | Suggested Range |
| --- | --- |
| Daily operations | Today |
| Weekly team meeting | Last 7 days |
| Monthly performance review | Current month |
| Campaign follow-up review | Campaign operating period |
| Performance comparison | Equivalent periods of the same length |

<Tip>
  Use the same date range when comparing performance over time. Comparing a seven-day period with an entire month can produce misleading conclusions.
</Tip>

---

## Total Members

**Total Members** shows how many sales-team members are represented in the overview.

Use this metric to confirm:

- Whether expected team members are present
- Whether recent team additions are reflected
- Whether the overview includes the expected team size
- Whether member access or setup requires review

If the number is lower than expected, open **Team Members** and confirm that the missing person has been added to the workspace and configured correctly.

---

## Total Calls Made

**Total Calls Made** shows the total number of calls recorded for the supported reporting period.

This value can help you understand:

- Overall team calling activity
- Whether assigned contacts are being actioned
- Changes in call volume over time
- Whether team activity aligns with operational expectations

A high call count does not automatically indicate successful performance. Review completed calls, outcomes, and conversion information alongside total call volume.

---

## Completed Calls

**Completed Calls** shows the number of calls classified as completed.

Compare this value with total calls to identify whether a significant number of calls remain incomplete or unsuccessful.

A difference between total calls and completed calls may include activity such as:

- Calls that were not answered
- Calls that failed to connect
- Calls still being processed
- Calls that ended without a completed outcome
- Pending assigned contacts

<Note>
  A completed call is not necessarily a converted lead. It means that the call reached its completed processing state. Review call outcomes and member details to understand the business result.
</Note>

---

## Average Calls per Member

**Avg Calls / Member** distributes the total call volume across the number of team members.

This metric provides a quick indication of overall workload distribution.

Use it to identify:

- Whether activity is evenly distributed
- Whether overall call volume is increasing or decreasing
- Whether additional assignments may be required
- Whether individual performance should be reviewed

The average should not be used by itself to evaluate an individual. Team members may have different roles, assignments, schedules, contact volumes, and call complexities.

---

## Conversion Rate

**Conversion Rate** presents the overall conversion percentage returned by the sales-team overview.

Use it together with:

- Total calls made
- Completed calls
- Call outcomes
- Assigned contacts
- Individual member performance
- Conversation details

A conversion rate can be influenced by several factors:

- Contact-list quality
- Customer availability
- Salesperson follow-up
- Call timing
- Product relevance
- Customer intent
- Geographic or language suitability
- Number of completed calls

<Warning>
  Do not evaluate team members using conversion rate alone. A small number of calls can produce a high percentage, while a member with more difficult or less-qualified contacts may produce a lower percentage despite strong activity.
</Warning>

## Assigned vs Completed Calls

The **Assigned vs Completed Calls** chart compares assigned calling work with completed activity for individual team members.

<Frame>
  ![Assign Call](/images/assign-call.png "Assign Call")
</Frame>

The chart contains two measures:

| Measure | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Assigned** | The member's assigned call target or contact workload |
| **Completed** | Calls completed by that member |

The chart displays the highest-ranked available members, up to the number supported by the dashboard.

### How to interpret the chart

- A small difference between assigned and completed activity can indicate that the member is progressing through the assigned workload.
- A large difference can indicate pending work, unsuccessful contact attempts, a recent assignment, or an operational issue.
- A completed value close to the assigned value does not confirm conversion quality. Review outcomes and contact details as well.
- A value higher or lower than expected should be investigated using the member's detailed page.

<Info>
  This chart uses current daily target and completed-call information. It is not a historical total for every date in the selected date range.
</Info>

### Questions to investigate

When assigned and completed values differ significantly, check:

1. When the contacts were assigned
2. Whether the contact telephone numbers are valid
3. Whether calls failed or were not answered
4. Whether the member was available during the period
5. Whether follow-up calls are still pending
6. Whether the member's target is appropriate
7. Whether technical or account-access issues affected calling

## Workspace Call Outcomes

The **Workspace Call Outcomes** chart summarizes the distribution of call results across the sales workspace.

The outcome chart can contain:

| Outcome | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Qualified** | The contact met the applicable qualification criteria |
| **Not Interested** | The contact indicated that they were not interested |
| **Callback** | The contact requested or required a follow-up call |
| **No Outcome** | No final business outcome was recorded |

The center of the chart highlights the leading outcome and its percentage when outcome data is available.

### Using call outcomes

Use the outcome distribution to answer questions such as:

- What portion of calls are producing qualified opportunities?
- Are many contacts requesting callbacks?
- Is the team reaching unsuitable or uninterested contacts?
- Are calls frequently ending without an outcome?
- Does the contact list need improvement?
- Do salespeople require additional guidance?

<Note>
  Outcome percentages summarize available records. Always review the underlying conversations before making decisions about individual customers or team members.
</Note>

---

## Qualified

A **Qualified** outcome generally indicates that the contact met the relevant qualification requirements.

Before continuing with a sales action:

1. Open the responsible team member.
2. Locate the assigned contact.
3. Review the call details.
4. Confirm the customer's interest.
5. Check whether a follow-up was requested.
6. Verify important information against the transcript or source records.

---

## Not Interested

A **Not Interested** outcome means the customer indicated that the offer, product, service, or conversation was not relevant to them at that time.

Repeated Not Interested outcomes may indicate:

- Poor contact targeting
- An unsuitable customer segment
- An unclear introduction
- Incorrect timing
- An offer that does not match customer needs
- Insufficient information during the call

Do not automatically call the contact again unless your organization's process, consent status, and applicable communication rules permit it.

---

## Callback

A **Callback** outcome indicates that additional contact may be required.

When reviewing callback records:

- Confirm the requested date or time
- Check the customer's time zone
- Review the reason for the callback
- Assign responsibility for following up
- Avoid duplicate callbacks
- Record the result in the appropriate system

---

## No Outcome

**No Outcome** means that no final business outcome was recorded for the call.

This may happen when:

- The call did not connect
- The conversation ended early
- Processing was incomplete
- The customer did not provide a clear response
- Outcome analysis was unavailable
- Additional review is required

Review the associated call before deciding whether another attempt is appropriate.

---

## Calls in the Last 7 Days

The **Calls in the Last 7 Days** chart displays total call activity for each of the most recent seven days.

The chart:

- Shows one entry per day
- Displays the oldest day first
- Includes days with zero recorded calls
- Uses total calls as the measured value
- Always represents the latest seven-day period

Use this chart to identify:

- Sudden increases or decreases in call volume
- Days with no activity
- Consistent calling patterns
- Operational interruptions
- Differences between expected and actual activity

<Info>
  The Calls in the Last 7 Days chart always shows the latest seven days. Changing the overview date range does not convert this chart into a different historical period.
</Info>

### Investigating unusual trends

If the chart shows an unexpected decline:

1. Check whether team members had assigned contacts.
2. Review workspace or calling availability.
3. Confirm that team members could access their accounts.
4. Check for failed or incomplete calls.
5. Verify whether the decline occurred on a holiday or non-working day.
6. Open Team Members to review individual activity.

## Top Performers

The **Top Performers** table highlights leading team members and provides a direct link to their detailed activity.

<Frame>
  ![Top Perform](/images/top-perform.png "Top Perform")
</Frame>

The table contains:

| Column | What It Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Member** | Team member's name |
| **Assigned Contacts** | Number of contacts assigned to the member |
| **Calls Made** | Number of calls associated with the member |
| **Completed** | Number of completed calls |
| **Conversion Rate** | Completed calls as a percentage of calls made |
| **Actions** | Opens the member's detailed activity |

### Open a member

You can open the member's details in either of two ways:

- Click the team member's row
- Click the arrow button in the **Actions** column

The member detail page provides access to the member's assigned customers and recorded call activity.

If no team data is available, the table displays:

```text
No team members found
```

## How Team Overview Metrics Relate

The dashboard metrics should be interpreted together.

| If You See | Also Review |
| --- | --- |
| High calls but low completed calls | Call status, connectivity, contact quality, and member details |
| High completed calls but low conversion | Outcomes, contact targeting, and conversation quality |
| Many callback outcomes | Follow-up workload and requested callback times |
| Many Not Interested outcomes | Contact-list quality and offer relevance |
| Many No Outcome records | Call details, processing status, and conversation availability |
| Uneven member activity | Assigned contacts, member availability, and workload distribution |
| A sudden decline in calls | Assignments, calling access, working schedules, and technical status |

## Recommended Daily Review

Managers can use the following workflow for daily operational monitoring:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check total team activity">
    Review Total Calls Made and Completed Calls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review assigned versus completed work">
    Look for team members with a significant difference between assigned and completed activity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect workspace outcomes">
    Check whether Qualified, Callback, Not Interested, or No Outcome is the leading result.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the recent trend">
    Confirm that the latest daily call volume matches operational expectations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open notable performers">
    Review team members with unusually high or low activity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Investigate exceptions">
    Open the relevant member and customer records before taking action.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Recommended Weekly Review

For weekly performance reviews:

1. Use a consistent reporting period.
2. Compare total calls with completed calls.
3. Review average calls per member.
4. Check overall conversion.
5. Compare assigned and completed activity.
6. Review the call-outcome distribution.
7. Investigate days with unusually low activity.
8. Review top performers.
9. Identify members who may need additional assignments or assistance.
10. Use detailed call records to support your conclusions.

---

## Fair Performance Evaluation

Team Overview is an operational dashboard, not a complete employee evaluation system.

When reviewing an individual, consider:

- Number of assigned contacts
- Contact-list quality
- Calls made
- Calls completed
- Customer availability
- Call outcomes
- Follow-up workload
- Total talk time
- Role and responsibilities
- Time available during the reporting period
- Approved leave or schedule differences

<Warning>
  Do not use a single metric or automated outcome as the sole basis for employment, compensation, disciplinary, or other significant personnel decisions.
</Warning>

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Compare related metrics">
    Review calls made, completed calls, outcomes, and conversion together. One number rarely explains overall team performance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Use consistent date ranges">
    Use equivalent reporting periods when comparing one week or month with another.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Investigate before taking action">
    Open the team member and customer records before concluding that low activity represents poor performance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Review both volume and quality">
    High call volume is useful only when conversations are completed appropriately and produce meaningful outcomes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Monitor pending callbacks">
    A high number of callback outcomes can create a follow-up backlog. Make sure callbacks are assigned and completed at the requested time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Protect customer and employee information">
    Team performance and customer calling information should only be shared with authorized people.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The Team Overview page is empty">
    Confirm that sales-team members and calling activity exist in the current workspace. Also verify that you are signed into the correct workspace and have permission to view the Sales Team module.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Summary cards are not displayed">
    Summary cards require overview data. Refresh the page and try again. If the charts load but the summary cards do not, the overview information may be temporarily unavailable.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="One chart is empty while other charts work">
    Dashboard sections load from separate information sources. A chart can be empty when no data exists for its reporting period or when that specific information could not be loaded.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Changing the date range does not change the last-seven-days chart">
    This is expected. The Calls in the Last 7 Days chart always displays the latest seven days.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Changing the date range does not change assigned versus completed calls">
    This chart represents current daily assigned and completed activity. It does not use the selected historical date range.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The total member count is incorrect">
    Open Team Members and verify which users are included. Confirm that recently added members are correctly configured in the current workspace.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A member is missing from Top Performers">
    Top Performers is a selected performance list, not necessarily the complete team directory. Click View Team Members to locate the person in the full list.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Conversion is 0%">
    A zero conversion rate can mean that no applicable calls were completed or that the available calls did not satisfy the conversion calculation. Open the member details and review the underlying activity.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="No team members are found">
    Confirm that users have been configured for the Sales Team module. Workspace membership alone may not guarantee that sales-team performance data exists.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The page shows Failed to load overview">
    Refresh the page and confirm your internet connection. If the problem continues, contact your workspace administrator or Valuez AI support.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Privacy and Access

Team Overview can contain employee performance information and customer-activity data.

Workspace administrators should ensure that:

- Only authorized managers can review team-wide performance
- Individual records are accessed for legitimate business purposes
- Customer telephone numbers and conversation data are protected
- Performance information is not exported or shared unnecessarily
- Access is removed when a user's responsibilities change
- Local privacy, employment, and data-protection requirements are followed
