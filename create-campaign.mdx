The Create Campaign workflow guides you through configuring an outbound AI calling campaign.

A campaign combines an AI voice agent, caller number, contact list, calling rules, and launch schedule into one managed operation.

The current campaign wizard contains four stages:

1. **Campaign Information**
2. **Call Settings**
3. **Add Contacts**
4. **Schedule & Review**

<Warning>
  Do not launch a campaign until you have confirmed that your contact data, calling hours, customer consent, agent behavior, and caller number comply with your organization's policies and applicable laws.
</Warning>

---

## Before You Begin

Prepare the following before creating a campaign:

- An active Valuez AI subscription that supports campaigns
- Permission to create or edit campaigns
- A configured and tested AI voice agent
- A caller phone number
- A valid contact list
- The campaign's intended language
- The contact time zone
- Approved calling hours
- Expected daily call volume
- Sufficient credits
- A confirmed launch date and time

<Info>
  Maximum concurrent calls and maximum contacts per campaign can depend on your subscription plan.
</Info>

---

## Open Create Campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Call Campaigns">
    Select **Call Campaigns** from the main navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Create Campaign">
    Open **Create Campaign** from the Campaign Management section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the four stages">
    Enter campaign information, configure calling, add contacts, and review the launch schedule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or launch">
    Save the campaign as a draft or launch it after completing all required information.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Campaign Creation Workflow

```text
Campaign Information
        ↓
Call Settings
        ↓
Add Contacts
        ↓
Schedule & Review
        ↓
Save as Draft or Launch Campaign
```

Use **Back** and **Continue** to move between stages.

## Stage 1: Campaign Information

<Frame>
  ![Camp Info](/images/camp-info.png "Camp Info")
</Frame>

The first stage identifies the campaign and assigns its AI voice agent.

### Campaign information fields

| Field | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Campaign Name** | Yes | A recognizable name for the campaign |
| **AI Voice Agent** | Yes | The agent that will handle campaign calls |
| **Products to Offer** | No | Products relevant to the campaign |
| **Services to Offer** | No | Services relevant to the campaign |
| **Campaign Purpose** | No | Description of the campaign's objective |

---

## Campaign Name

Enter a descriptive campaign name.

The current field:

- Allows letters
- Allows numbers
- Allows spaces
- Allows hyphens
- Allows underscores
- Removes unsupported special characters
- Prevents leading spaces
- Prevents repeated spaces
- Supports up to 100 characters

Recommended format:

```text
[Objective] - [Audience] - [Period or Region]
```

Examples:

```text
Renewal Reminder - Existing Customers - October
Lead Qualification - Mumbai - Q4
Appointment Confirmation - Clinic A
Product Follow-Up - Trial Customers
```

---

## Select an AI Voice Agent

Select the agent that will conduct the campaign calls.

The selected agent determines important behavior, including:

- Greeting
- Conversation objective
- Supported languages
- Voice
- Tone
- Knowledge Base information
- Questions asked
- Response behavior
- Closing instructions

Only available agents appear in the selector.

<Warning>
  Test the selected agent before launching the campaign. A configuration that works for website conversations may still require changes for proactive outbound calls.
</Warning>

### Agent testing checklist

Confirm that the agent:

- Introduces itself appropriately
- Explains the purpose of the call
- Uses the required language
- Pronounces important names correctly
- Handles objections appropriately
- Does not make unsupported claims
- Protects sensitive information
- Records the expected outcome
- Ends conversations professionally

---

## Products and Services

Use **Products to Offer** and **Services to Offer** to associate campaign offerings with the calling workflow.

You can select multiple items and adjust their priority order.

The order can help indicate which products or services are most relevant to the campaign.

Before continuing:

- Remove unrelated items
- Place the most important item first
- Confirm that the agent has accurate information about every selected offering
- Ensure that outdated offerings are not included

---

## Campaign Purpose

Use Campaign Purpose to explain why the campaign is being conducted.

Example:

```text
Contact existing customers whose annual service plan expires this month,
explain the renewal options, answer basic questions, and identify customers
who want a follow-up from the sales team.
```

A useful purpose should describe:

- Target audience
- Reason for calling
- Desired outcome
- Important boundaries
- Required follow-up

<Note>
  Campaign Purpose does not replace the agent's complete prompt or Knowledge Base. It provides campaign-level context.
</Note>

---

## Stage 1 Validation

Before continuing, Valuez AI verifies that:

- Campaign Name is entered
- AI Voice Agent is selected

If required information is missing, the relevant field displays an error.

## Stage 2: Call Settings

<Frame>
  ![Camp Call Setting](/images/camp-call-setting.png "Camp Call Setting")
</Frame>

Call Settings determines how, when, and at what volume calls are placed.

### Call settings fields

| Field | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Caller Number** | Yes | Phone number used for outgoing calls |
| **Starting Language** | Yes | Initial language used by the agent |
| **Time Zone** | Yes | Time zone used to interpret calling hours |
| **Allowed Calling Hours** | Configured | Daily start and end time |
| **Maximum Concurrent Calls** | Yes | Maximum simultaneous campaign calls |
| **Daily Call Limit** | No | Maximum calls attempted in one day |
| **Retry Unanswered Calls** | No | Enables retry behavior |
| **Maximum Retries** | When retry enabled | Maximum repeat attempts |
| **Retry Interval** | When retry enabled | Hours between retry attempts |

---

## Caller Number

Select the phone number that customers will see when the campaign calls them.

Only available campaign caller numbers appear in the selector.

Before selecting a number, confirm:

- It belongs to the correct workspace
- It is active
- It supports the required calling destination
- It is connected to the correct provider
- It is appropriate for the target audience

If no caller number appears, configure one from **Phone Numbers** or contact your workspace administrator.

---

## Starting Language

Select the language in which the agent should begin the call.

The available choices are limited to languages enabled for the selected AI agent.

If the required language is missing:

1. Return to AI Voice Agents
2. Open the selected agent
3. Review its language configuration
4. Add the required supported language
5. Test the agent
6. Return to the campaign

<Info>
  Starting Language determines how the call begins. The agent's behavior after that depends on its supported-language configuration.
</Info>

---

## Time Zone

Select the time zone used for campaign scheduling and allowed calling hours.

The time zone is important because customers may be located in a different region from the campaign operator.

Before launch, verify:

- Where the contacts are located
- Whether one time zone is appropriate for the complete list
- Whether separate campaigns are needed for different regions
- That the launch time is correct in the selected zone

---

## Allowed Calling Hours

Set the earliest and latest time during which campaign calls may be placed.

Example:

```text
Start: 10:00
End:   18:00
```

The end time must be at least one hour after the start time.

If you change the start time to a value that makes the current end time invalid, the interface adjusts the end time to maintain the minimum interval.

<Warning>
  Platform calling hours do not replace your legal or contractual responsibilities. Confirm permitted calling times for the contact's jurisdiction and consent status.
</Warning>

---

## Maximum Concurrent Calls

Maximum Concurrent Calls controls how many calls the campaign can process at the same time.

For example:

```text
Maximum Concurrent Calls: 5
```

This allows up to five simultaneous calls, subject to platform capacity and provider behavior.

The field displays your current plan limit.

You cannot configure a value higher than the concurrency supported by your subscription.

### Choosing a concurrency value

Consider:

- Available credits
- Contact-list size
- Campaign urgency
- Human follow-up capacity
- Transfer-team availability
- External provider capacity
- Monitoring capacity during launch

<Tip>
  Start with a lower concurrency value for a new agent or campaign. Increase it only after reviewing successful calls, failures, transcripts, and credit consumption.
</Tip>

---

## Daily Call Limit

Daily Call Limit restricts the maximum number of calls the campaign can attempt per day.

Use it to:

- Control campaign volume
- Limit credit consumption
- Match sales-team capacity
- Avoid contacting too many customers at once
- Spread calls across several days

The field accepts numeric values.

<Note>
  Daily Call Limit controls campaign attempt volume. It does not guarantee that every attempted call will connect or complete.
</Note>

---

## Retry Unanswered Calls

Enable **Retry unanswered calls** when the campaign should attempt another call after an unanswered attempt.

When enabled, configure:

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Maximum Retries** | Maximum number of additional attempts |
| **Retry Interval (Hours)** | Time between attempts |

Example:

```text
Maximum Retries: 2
Retry Interval: 24 hours
```

Use retries carefully to avoid excessive contact attempts.

Recommended considerations:

- Customer consent
- Local calling rules
- Campaign urgency
- Appropriate spacing between attempts
- Maximum total attempts
- Whether unsuccessful numbers should be removed

---

## Stage 2 Validation

Before continuing, Valuez AI checks that:

- A Caller Number is selected
- The Starting Language is enabled for the selected agent
- A Time Zone is selected
- Maximum Concurrent Calls is within the plan limit

If any requirement is missing or invalid, correct the highlighted field before continuing.

## Stage 3: Add Contacts

<Frame>
  ![Camp Call Setting](/images/camp-call-setting.png "Camp Call Setting")
</Frame>

Contacts can be added in two ways:

- Upload a CSV or Excel file
- Add contacts manually

At least one contact is required before launch.

---

## Upload a Contact File

Valuez AI supports:

- CSV files
- Excel files

You can drag a file into the upload area or browse for it.

### Required contact information

The uploaded file must include a phone-number column.

Recognized phone-column names include forms such as:

```text
ContactNumber
Mobile Number
```

A customer-name column is strongly recommended for personalized calls.

Example:

| CustomerName | ContactNumber | Email | City |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Priya Sharma | 9876543210 | [priya@example.com](mailto:priya@example.com) | Mumbai |
| Rahul Mehta | 9823456789 | [rahul@example.com](mailto:rahul@example.com) | Pune |

### Recommended file columns

| Column | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **ContactNumber** | Required |
| **CustomerName** | Recommended |
| **AlternateContactNumber** | Optional |
| **Email** | Optional |
| **Address** | Optional |
| **City** | Optional |
| **District** | Optional |
| **State** | Optional |
| **Country** | Optional |
| **Pincode** | Optional |
| **Source** | Optional |

<Warning>
  Remove formulas, merged cells, decorative headings, totals, and empty introductory rows before uploading the file.
</Warning>

---

## Contact Limits

The upload area displays the maximum number of contacts allowed per campaign file under your current plan.

If the file exceeds the limit:

- Divide the contacts into smaller approved batches
- Remove unnecessary records
- Upgrade the plan if appropriate

Do not bypass operational or consent requirements by dividing unauthorized contact data across files.

---

## Uploaded Documents

After an upload, Valuez AI displays the uploaded document and the number of contacts associated with it.

You can remove an uploaded document.

Removing an uploaded document also removes the contacts associated with that import after confirmation.

<Warning>
  Review the document name and contact count carefully before confirming removal.
</Warning>

---

## Add a Contact Manually

Select **Add Manually** to enter an individual contact.

The manual form supports:

| Field | Required |
| --- | --- |
| Customer Name | Yes |
| Contact Number | Yes |
| Alternate Contact Number | No |
| Email | No |
| Address | No |
| Country | Yes |
| State | Yes |
| City | No |
| District | No |
| Pincode | No |
| Source | Yes |
| Other Source | Required when Source is Other |

Current source options include:

- Website
- Referral
- Walk-in
- Field Agent
- Social Media
- Other

### Current phone validation

The current manual-contact form expects:

- A 10-digit primary mobile number
- A first digit between 6 and 9
- Digits only
- The same format for an optional alternate number

The current location selector is configured for India and its states and cities.

<Info>
  For contacts outside the supported manual-entry format, use an approved import method only if the backend and calling provider support the required destination format.
</Info>

---

## Review Contact Records

The contact table lets you:

- Search contacts by name
- Select individual contacts
- Select all contacts displayed on the current page
- Review phone numbers
- Review contact status
- Edit a contact
- Delete a contact
- Move through contact pages

Only selected contacts are mapped to the campaign when you continue.

### Edit a contact

Use Edit to correct:

- Name
- Phone number
- Alternate number
- Email
- Address
- Location
- Source

### Delete a contact

Use Delete to remove an individual contact from the campaign.

Deletion requires confirmation.

<Warning>
  Verify that you selected the correct contact. Deleting a campaign contact cannot be assumed to delete or modify the original record in every external system.
</Warning>

---

## Contact Data Quality Checklist

Before continuing, confirm:

- [ ] Every selected contact has a valid phone number
- [ ] Duplicate phone numbers have been removed
- [ ] Names are correctly mapped
- [ ] Country and location data are accurate
- [ ] Contacts belong to the intended audience
- [ ] Customers have the required consent
- [ ] Suppression and do-not-call requirements have been applied
- [ ] Test or internal numbers are identified
- [ ] Selected-contact count is correct

## Stage 4: Schedule & Review

<Frame>
  ![Camp Call Sche](/images/camp-call-sche.png "Camp Call Sche")
</Frame>

The final stage sets the campaign launch time and summarizes the configuration.

### Required launch fields

| Field | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Start Date** | Yes | Date on which the campaign can begin |
| **Start Time** | Yes | Time at which the campaign can begin |

Past dates cannot be selected through the date picker.

You can use **Today** and **Now** when an immediate or same-day launch is appropriate.

---

## Campaign Summary

Before launch, review:

| Summary Item | What to Verify |
| --- | --- |
| **Campaign** | Correct campaign name |
| **Agent** | Correct, tested AI voice agent |
| **Contacts** | Correct selected-contact count |
| **Calling Hours** | Correct permitted time window |
| **Retries** | Appropriate retry behavior |
| **Estimated Call Minutes** | Expected campaign calling duration |
| **Estimated Credits** | Expected credit requirement |
| **Available Credits** | Current available balance |
| **Launch Checklist** | Whether required configuration is complete |

<Warning>
  Estimated minutes and credits are planning values. Actual consumption can vary based on connected calls, conversation duration, retries, and provider behavior.
</Warning>

---

## Save as Draft

Use **Save as Draft** when the campaign is not ready to launch.

Saving as draft is appropriate when:

- Contact data needs review
- Calling settings require approval
- Agent testing is incomplete
- Credits are insufficient
- Launch timing is undecided
- Compliance review is pending

After saving, the campaign appears in the **Drafts** tab.

You can reopen it later and continue configuration.

---

## Launch Campaign

Before selecting **Launch Campaign**, confirm:

- Campaign name is correct
- AI agent is correct
- Agent has been tested
- Caller number is active
- Starting language is supported
- Time zone is correct
- Calling hours are permitted
- Concurrency is within the plan limit
- Daily limit is appropriate
- Retry settings are appropriate
- Selected contacts are valid
- Launch date and time are correct
- Sufficient credits are available

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the complete summary">
    Confirm the agent, contacts, calling rules, estimates, and available credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a start date">
    Choose today or a future date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a start time">
    Choose the intended campaign start time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Launch Campaign">
    Valuez AI validates the launch configuration and saves the completed campaign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor the campaign">
    Return to Campaigns Overview and review its status, calls, and leads.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## After Launch

After the campaign is launched:

1. Confirm that it appears in Campaigns Overview
2. Check whether its status is Scheduled, Starting, Active, or Running
3. Monitor early call attempts
4. Review failed or unanswered calls
5. Listen to representative recordings
6. Read transcripts
7. Review call outcomes
8. Monitor credit usage
9. Pause the campaign if unexpected behavior occurs

<Tip>
  For a new agent or calling workflow, review the first small group of completed calls before allowing the campaign to continue at full volume.
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No AI agents are available">
    Create and configure an AI voice agent first. Confirm that you have permission to use it and that it is available in the current workspace.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="No caller numbers are available">
    Open Phone Numbers and configure an eligible calling number, or contact your workspace administrator.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The required language is missing">
    Add the language to the selected agent's configuration and test the agent before returning to the campaign.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Maximum Concurrent Calls is rejected">
    Enter a positive value within the limit displayed for your subscription plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The contact file does not upload">
    Confirm that the file is CSV or Excel, contains a header row, contains a supported phone-number column, and does not exceed the plan's contact limit.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Contacts are required">
    Upload or manually add contacts, then select the contacts that should be mapped to the campaign.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A manual phone number is rejected">
    The current manual form requires a 10-digit Indian mobile number beginning with 6, 7, 8, or 9.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The campaign cannot be launched">
    Confirm that the required information is complete, at least one contact is selected, concurrency is valid, and the start date and time are present.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The campaign was saved as a draft">
    Open the Drafts tab, edit the campaign, complete the missing stages, and launch it when ready.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Estimated credits exceed available credits">
    Reduce the contact list or retry settings, add credits, or upgrade the subscription before launching.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
