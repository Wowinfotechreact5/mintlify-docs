Campaign Leads brings together the customer and qualification information collected through outbound campaigns.

Use this area to:

- Review all campaign leads
- Identify interested and qualified leads
- Track conversions and voicemail outcomes
- Review the most recent contact time
- Open a lead's complete profile
- Read AI-generated call summaries
- Listen to available recordings
- Review call transcripts
- Track lead activity
- Add internal follow-up notes

<Info>
  Campaign Leads is a reporting and follow-up area. Contacts are initially added during campaign creation, while lead information and outcomes are updated from campaign activity.
</Info>

---

## Open Campaign Leads

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Call Campaigns">
    Select **Call Campaigns** from the main navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Campaign Leads">
    Open **Campaign Leads** from the Contacts section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a lead category">
    Use the lead tabs to view all leads or focus on a specific outcome.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a lead">
    Select a lead row or the arrow action to view its complete details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understanding Call Outcomes

A lead's Call Outcome summarizes the result associated with campaign activity.

The current interface supports outcomes including:

| Outcome | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Interested** | The customer expressed interest |
| **Not Interested** | The customer did not express interest |
| **Qualified** | The lead met the configured qualification conditions |
| **No Outcome** | No conclusive business outcome was captured |
| **Voicemail** | The call reached voicemail |
| **Converted** | The lead was recorded as converted |
| **Completed** | The interaction was recorded as completed |
| **Failed** | The call or interaction failed |

Additional values may be displayed when returned by the campaign system.

<Warning>
  A call outcome is an operational classification. Confirm important qualification or conversion decisions using the transcript, recording, AI summary, and your organization's sales process.
</Warning>

## Qualified Leads

A qualified lead is a contact that met the qualification conditions captured during the AI conversation.

Qualification can depend on information such as:

- Customer requirement
- Product or service interest
- Budget
- Purchase timeline
- Location
- Eligibility
- Requested follow-up
- Other campaign-specific answers

The exact criteria depend on the agent prompt and campaign workflow.

### Review a qualified lead

Before assigning or contacting a qualified lead:

1. Open the lead details
2. Review the AI Call Summary
3. Read the Qualification Reason
4. Review captured key details
5. Read the transcript
6. Listen to the recording when available
7. Confirm contact information
8. Check the preferred callback time
9. Add an internal note if follow-up is required

---

## Interested and Converted Leads

### Interested

Interested indicates that the lead expressed interest during the interaction.

An interested lead may still require:

- Additional qualification
- Product clarification
- Pricing information
- A demonstration
- A callback
- Human sales follow-up

### Converted

Converted indicates that the lead has been classified as converted by the campaign's operational process.

<Info>
  “Converted” does not necessarily mean payment has been received. Use your CRM, billing system, or internal process as the final commercial record.
</Info>

---

## Voicemail and No Outcome

### Voicemail

Voicemail indicates that the call reached a voicemail system instead of completing a normal customer conversation.

Depending on campaign settings, the contact may be retried.

### No Outcome

No Outcome means the platform does not have a conclusive business result for that lead.

Possible reasons include:

- Call was unanswered
- Customer ended the call early
- Conversation did not reach qualification
- Required information was not captured
- Call failed
- Follow-up is still required

Review the detailed call information before deciding the next action.

## AI Call Summary

The AI Call Summary can include:

### Customer Needs

A summary of what the customer wants or requires.

### Qualification Reason

An explanation of why the lead was classified as qualified or otherwise categorized.

### Key Details Captured

Structured information collected during the conversation.

The exact keys depend on the campaign and agent.

Examples might include:

```text
Product Interest
Budget
Preferred Date
Location
Business Type
Required Quantity
Callback Preference
```

<Warning>
  AI-generated summaries can be incomplete or inaccurate. Verify important details using the full transcript and recording.
</Warning>

## View Transcript

Select **View Transcript** from the AI Call Summary to open the Conversation tab.

This allows you to compare the summary with the complete interaction.

Use the transcript when:

- Qualification appears incorrect
- Important information is missing
- A customer disputes what was discussed
- Follow-up teams need exact context
- The agent behaved unexpectedly

## Conversation Tab

<Frame>
  ![Camp Conversation](/images/camp-conversation.png "Camp Conversation")
</Frame>

The Conversation tab can contain:

- Call recording
- Caller messages
- AI agent messages
- Message timestamps
- Sentiment information, when available
- Emotion information, when available

### Call recording

When recording information exists, an audio player appears above the transcript.

Use it to:

- Verify exact wording
- Review customer tone
- Check agent pronunciation
- Compare the audio with the transcript
- Investigate a disputed interaction

The recording section is hidden when no recording is available.

### Transcript

Transcript entries identify the speaker as:

- **Caller / User**
- **AI Agent**

If a transcript cannot be retrieved, the page indicates that no transcript is available.

<Note>
  Speech recognition may interpret names, numbers, accents, or background noise incorrectly. Use the recording as the primary reference when exact wording matters.
</Note>

## Activity Tab

The Activity tab shows important recorded lead events.

Events can include:

- Lead marked as qualified
- Outbound call completed
- Follow-up scheduled
- Other timeline events returned for the lead

Each event includes a date and time when available.

Use the timeline to understand what happened and in what order.

<Info>
  Activity is an operational history. It does not necessarily include activity performed in external CRM, email, calendar, or billing systems.
</Info>

## Notes Tab

<Frame>
  ![Camp Notes](/images/camp-notes.png "Camp Notes")
</Frame>

The Notes tab allows authorized team members to add internal notes about a lead.

Examples include:

```text
Customer requested a callback after 3:00 PM on Friday.
```

```text
Interested in the premium plan but needs pricing approval.
```

```text
Follow-up assigned to the regional sales team.
```

### Add a note

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Notes">
    Select the **Notes** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the note">
    Write clear, relevant follow-up information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the note">
    Select **Add Note**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also use:

```text
Ctrl + Enter
```

or on macOS:

```text
Command + Enter
```

to submit a note.

Each saved note displays its creation date and time.

<Warning>
  Do not enter passwords, payment-card information, authentication secrets, API keys, or unnecessary sensitive personal information in lead notes.
</Warning>

## Recommended Lead Review Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start with qualified leads">
    Open the Qualified tab and review the most recent leads.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the lead profile">
    Confirm name, phone number, location, campaign, and callback information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the AI summary">
    Read the requirement, qualification reason, and key details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the conversation">
    Compare the AI summary with the transcript and recording.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check activity">
    Review call completion, qualification, and follow-up events.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a note">
    Record the next action, assignment, or important context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete follow-up">
    Use your organization's approved sales or support process.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Lead Quality Checklist

Before treating a lead as sales-ready, confirm:

- [ ] Customer identity is sufficiently clear
- [ ] Phone number is valid
- [ ] Requirement is understood
- [ ] Product or service interest is relevant
- [ ] Qualification reason is supported by the conversation
- [ ] Lead score is interpreted according to your process
- [ ] Preferred callback time is recorded
- [ ] Important details were verified
- [ ] Transcript and summary are consistent
- [ ] Consent and contact requirements are satisfied
- [ ] Next action is assigned
- [ ] An internal note has been added when necessary

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Verify AI qualification">
    Do not rely only on the Qualified label. Review the qualification reason and conversation evidence before assigning high-priority follow-up.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Prioritize recent qualified leads">
    Review recently contacted qualified leads quickly while the interaction is still relevant.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Respect callback preferences">
    When a preferred callback is available, contact the customer within the requested period and permitted calling hours.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Write useful notes">
    Record specific next actions, commitments, requested information, and ownership. Avoid vague notes such as “call later.”
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Protect personal information">
    Restrict lead access to authorized team members and avoid copying customer information into unapproved tools.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Review failed and no-outcome leads">
    These records can reveal invalid numbers, campaign configuration problems, poor timing, or unclear agent behavior.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No leads are displayed">
    Confirm that campaigns contain contacts and have produced campaign activity. Select the All tab and refresh the page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A lead appears under the wrong category">
    Open the lead and review the latest outcome, AI summary, transcript, and activity. The category is based on stored campaign outcome data.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Last Contact is empty">
    The lead may not have a completed or recorded call yet.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Lead details do not open">
    Return to Campaign Leads and open the record again. If the issue continues, record the Lead ID and campaign name when reporting the problem.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The AI summary is missing">
    A summary may be unavailable when the call did not complete or did not produce enough conversation data.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="No recording is available">
    The interaction may not have produced recording data, or the recording may still be processing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The transcript is unavailable">
    Review the call outcome and recording. The calling provider or speech-processing workflow may not have returned transcript data.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A note cannot be added">
    Confirm that the note contains text and that the lead still belongs to the campaign. Refresh the page and try again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The displayed count does not match the current page">
    Tab and summary counts represent the overall stored lead data, while the table may show only the current paginated page.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
