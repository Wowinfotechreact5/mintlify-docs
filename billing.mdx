The **Billing** page brings together your workspace’s subscription summary, available credit balance, payment records, billing identity, tax information, and invoicing address.

Use this page to:

- Review your active plan
- Open the available subscription plans
- Check your current credit balance
- Purchase additional credits
- Open detailed credit-usage logs
- Review previous payments
- Check payment status
- Download available payment receipts
- Add or update billing details
- Maintain GST and tax information
- Control the legal information used on invoices

<Info>
  Billing information belongs to the active Valuez AI workspace. If your account can access multiple workspaces, confirm that the correct workspace is active before changing billing information or making a purchase.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to Valuez AI">
    Sign in using an account that has access to the required workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Open **Billing** from the dashboard navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the workspace summary">
    The top of the page displays your active plan and available credit balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a billing section">
    Select **Payment history** or **Billing details** to review the corresponding information.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Billing Overview

The top of the Billing page contains two summary cards.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Current Plan">
    The first card identifies the plan associated with the active workspace.

    Depending on the plan, it can display:

    - Pay as you go
    - Silver Plan
    - Gold Plan
    - Platinum Plan
    - Another plan name returned for the workspace
    - Active status

    The **View plans** button opens the Subscription & Plans page.

    <Note>
      The Basic plan is presented as **Pay as you go** on the Billing page.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Credit Balance">
    The second card displays the workspace’s currently available credit balance.

    From this card, you can:

    - Select **Add credits** to purchase additional credits
    - Select **View usage logs** to review detailed credit activity

    The displayed balance is formatted to two decimal places.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

---

## Current Plan Summary

The current-plan card provides a quick billing status for the workspace.

| Information | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Plan name** | The subscription currently associated with the workspace |
| **Active badge** | Indicates that the displayed plan is active |
| **Plan description** | A short explanation of the current subscription level |
| **View plans** | Opens the complete Subscription & Plans page |

For paid plans, the page indicates that the workspace is subscribed to higher-tier rate limits, custom agents, and priority voice queues.

For Basic, the page identifies the workspace as using the pay-as-you-go level and recommends reviewing paid plans for higher limits.

### Review other plans

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the current-plan card">
    Locate the plan summary at the top of the Billing page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select View plans">
    Click **View plans**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare available plans">
    Valuez AI opens the Subscription & Plans page, where you can compare plan limits, pricing, credits, and included features.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For detailed plan information, see [Subscription & Plans](/subscription-plans).

## Available Credit Balance

The credit-balance card displays the credits currently available to your workspace.

Credits can be consumed by supported Valuez AI operations, depending on your plan and the services used.

Examples of credit-consuming activity can include:

- Voice-agent conversations
- Speech processing
- API operations
- Outbound campaign calls
- Inbound calls
- Other metered AI services

<Warning>
  Credit consumption can vary according to the services used. Do not estimate remaining capacity only from the number of calls or interactions.
</Warning>

### Open credit usage logs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Navigate to the Billing page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the credit-balance card">
    Review the currently available credit balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select View usage logs">
    Click **View usage logs**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review credit activity">
    Valuez AI opens the Credit Usage page, where you can inspect detailed credit activity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Billing Sections

The Billing page has two main tabs:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Payment History">
    Displays previous payment records and available receipt downloads.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Billing Details">
    Displays the legal identity, tax details, billing contact, and registered billing address used for receipts and invoices.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The selected tab is stored in the page URL. This means a direct Billing link can open either Payment History or Billing Details.

The **Payment history** tab lists billing transactions associated with your Valuez AI account.

The table contains the following columns:

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **No.** | Sequential record number for the current page |
| **Plan Name** | Plan associated with the transaction |
| **Receipt Number** | Unique receipt or invoice reference |
| **Amount** | Transaction amount displayed in Indian rupees |
| **Status** | Current payment status |
| **Actions** | Provides the receipt PDF download when available |

## Payment Statuses

A payment record can display one of the following statuses:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Success">
    The payment record was completed successfully.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Paid">
    The transaction is recorded as paid.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Pending">
    The payment has not yet reached a final successful or failed state.

    Wait before attempting the same payment again.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Failed">
    The payment did not complete successfully.

    Check whether any amount was deducted before making another payment.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

If no status is available for a record, the current interface displays it as **Pending**.

<Warning>
  A Pending status does not automatically mean that the payment failed. Avoid submitting duplicate payments until the original transaction reaches a final state.
</Warning>

---

## Sort Payment Amounts

The **Amount** column includes a sorting control.

Select the sort indicator to alternate between:

- Ascending amount order
- Descending amount order

The default payment-history sorting direction is descending.

<Note>
  Sorting applies to the payment records displayed by the table. Pagination may still divide the complete payment history across multiple pages.
</Note>

---

## Payment History Pagination

The Payment History table loads up to ten records per page in the current interface.

If more than one page is available, pagination controls appear beneath the table.

Use the pagination controls to:

- Open the next page
- Return to the previous page
- Move to another available page

The row number is calculated using the current page and page size, so numbering continues across pages.

## Download a Receipt

When a receipt PDF is available, the **Actions** column displays **Download PDF**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Payment history">
    Select the **Payment history** tab on the Billing page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the transaction">
    Locate the transaction using its plan name, receipt number, amount, or status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Download PDF">
    Click **Download PDF** in the Actions column.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the receipt">
    The receipt opens in a new browser tab when a valid receipt URL is available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the document">
    Use your browser’s download or print controls to save a local copy if required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  The Download PDF control works only when the transaction includes a valid receipt or invoice URL.
</Info>

<Warning>
  Confirm that the receipt opens from the official Valuez AI billing interface before downloading or sharing it.
</Warning>

---

## No Payment History

If the workspace has no available payment records, the table displays:

**No payment history found**

This can occur when:

- The workspace has not completed a paid transaction
- The workspace currently uses the Basic plan
- A recent payment has not yet been added to the billing history
- You are viewing a different workspace
- Payment records could not be retrieved

If you recently completed a payment, refresh the page after a short wait.

## Edit Billing Details

When billing details already exist, the page displays an **Edit Details** button.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing Details">
    Select the **Billing details** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Edit Details">
    Click **Edit Details** in the billing-profile header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the required fields">
    Correct the business, contact, tax, or address information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the updated profile">

**Billing details updated successfully!**

## Billing Detail Validation

Valuez AI validates key fields before saving billing information.

| Field | Validation behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Legal / Company Name** | Cannot be empty |
| **Billing Email** | Cannot be empty and must use a valid email format |
| **Mobile Number** | Cannot be empty |
| **Street Address** | Cannot be empty |
| **Country** | Must be selected |
| **Postal Code / PIN** | Cannot be empty |
| **GSTIN** | If provided, must match the valid 15-character GSTIN structure |
| **PAN / Tax ID** | Accepts up to 10 characters |
| **Mobile Number input** | Accepts up to 10 digits and must begin with 6, 7, 8, or 9 |

GSTIN and PAN or Tax ID values are converted to uppercase while you enter them.

<Warning>
  The Billing Details form checks that the mobile field is not empty, but you should still enter a complete and accurate 10-digit mobile number.
</Warning>

## Country, State, and City

The current Billing Details form limits the country selection to **India**.

After selecting India:

1. The State or Province list becomes available.
2. Selecting a state loads the corresponding city list.
3. If no predefined cities are available, the city can be entered manually.

Changing the country clears the previously selected state and city.

Changing the state clears the previously selected city.

<Tip>
  Select the country and state before entering the city to avoid having the city value cleared.
</Tip>

---

## GSTIN

GSTIN is optional in the Billing Details form.

If provided, it must follow the standard 15-character GSTIN structure.

An example format displayed by the application is:

```text
27AAAAA0000A1Z5
```

The field:

- Accepts up to 15 characters
- Converts letters to uppercase
- Rejects values that do not match the expected format

<Warning>
  The documentation example demonstrates formatting only. Do not use it as your organisation’s GSTIN.
</Warning>

---

## PAN or Tax ID

The **PAN / Tax ID** field is optional.

The field:

- Accepts up to 10 characters
- Converts letters to uppercase
- Appears on the saved billing profile when provided

Confirm the correct tax identifier with your organisation’s finance or accounting team.

The billing email can be different from the account sign-in email.

- Payment communication

- Subscription questions
- Credit-purchase records

Examples include a finance, billing, or accounts-payable address.

## Add Credits

The Billing page provides an **Add credits** button beside the available credit balance.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Navigate to the Billing page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Add credits">
    Click **Add credits** in the credit-balance card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the top-up amount">
    Enter the amount you want to purchase or select an available preset.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the credit quantity">
    The top-up interface uses the displayed conversion of ₹1 to 1 credit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review billing information">

  </Step>
  <Step title="Review GST and total">
    Confirm the base amount, GST amount, and final payment amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Proceed to payment">
    Click the payment button and complete the transaction through the hosted payment page.

## Credit Top-Up Amounts

The current Add Credits form supports:

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Minimum top-up** | ₹100 |
| **Maximum top-up** | ₹10,00,000 |
| **Conversion shown by the interface** | ₹1 = 1 credit |
| **GST** | Calculated separately |
| **Payment method** | Completed through the hosted payment flow |

The final total is calculated as:

```text
Base top-up amount + applicable GST = Total payment
```

<Info>
  The number entered as the top-up amount represents both the base rupee amount and the number of credits before tax.
</Info>

## After Purchasing Credits

When the Add Credits window closes, the Billing page requests refreshed credit information.

If the payment completed successfully:

1. Wait for payment processing to finish.
2. Return to the Billing page.
3. Refresh the page if necessary.
4. Confirm that the available credit balance has increased.
5. Open **View usage logs** to review related credit activity.
6. Open **Payment history** to look for the payment record and receipt.

<Note>
  A payment can take time to appear if the payment provider has not yet returned a final transaction status.
</Note>

## Receipt and Invoice Safety

Follow these practices when working with billing documents:

- Download receipts only from the Valuez AI Billing page
- Confirm the workspace and receipt number
- Check the plan name and transaction amount
- Store downloaded documents securely
- Avoid sharing receipts publicly
- Hide tax identifiers when sharing screenshots
- Never share payment credentials or OTPs
- Confirm that the payment page uses a secure HTTPS connection

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The Billing page does not load">
    Refresh the page and confirm that your internet connection is working.

    If the problem continues:

    1. Sign out and sign in again.
    2. Confirm that the correct workspace is active.
    3. Try another supported browser.
    4. Contact Valuez AI support with a screenshot.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The displayed plan is incorrect">
    Confirm that you are viewing the correct workspace.

    Open Subscription & Plans and refresh the plan information. If you recently completed a purchase, allow time for payment confirmation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The available credit balance is incorrect">
    Refresh the Billing page and open the Credit Usage page.

    If you recently purchased credits, check Payment History for the transaction status before contacting support.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="No payment history is displayed">
    Payment history may be empty if no paid transactions exist for the account or workspace.

    Also confirm that:

    - You are viewing the correct workspace
    - The payment completed successfully
    - The payment is no longer pending
    - The page finished loading
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Download PDF does not open anything">
    A valid receipt URL may not yet be available for that transaction.

    Confirm that the payment status is successful or paid, refresh the page, and try again later.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The receipt opens but does not download">
    The receipt opens in a new browser tab. Use the browser’s download or print control to save it.

    Check whether the browser blocked the new tab.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Billing details do not load">
    Refresh the page and confirm that the correct workspace is active.

    If the details still do not load, you can re-enter them only after confirming the correct legal and tax information.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot save billing details">
    Check every required field:

    - Legal or company name
    - Billing email
    - Mobile number
    - Street address
    - Country
    - Postal code

    Correct any field highlighted with an error and try again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My GSTIN is rejected">
    Enter a valid 15-character GSTIN without spaces or punctuation.

    Confirm the value with your organisation’s official tax registration record.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My mobile number is rejected">
    Enter an Indian mobile number using digits only.

    The number can contain up to 10 digits and must begin with 6, 7, 8, or 9.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The state or city list is empty">
    Select India as the country first, then select the state.

    If a city list is unavailable, enter the city manually when the text field appears.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Add credits does not complete">
    Verify all billing fields and check your internet connection.

    If the hosted payment page does not open, confirm that the browser permits redirects and pop-ups from Valuez AI.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Payment succeeded but credits are missing">
    Do not immediately repeat the purchase.

    Instead:

    1. Refresh the Billing page.
    2. Review Payment History.
    3. Open Credit Usage.
    4. Record the payment reference.
    5. Contact support if the balance remains unchanged.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The payment status is Pending">
    Wait for the payment provider to complete processing.

    Avoid making the same payment again until the first transaction has reached a final status.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The payment status is Failed">
    Check whether any amount was deducted.

    If an amount was deducted despite a Failed status, keep the transaction reference and contact support before retrying.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Before Contacting Support

Prepare the following information:

- Workspace or organisation name
- Account email address
- Billing email address
- Current plan
- Receipt number
- Payment reference
- Transaction amount
- Approximate transaction date and time
- Payment status
- Description of the problem
- Screenshot of the error
- Whether money was deducted
- Whether credits were added

<Warning>
  Never share your complete card number, CVV, OTP, bank password, UPI PIN, or Valuez AI password.
</Warning>
