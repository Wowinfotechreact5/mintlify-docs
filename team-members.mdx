Team Members provides a detailed view of the people performing sales-calling activities in your Valuez AI workspace.

Use this page to find a team member, review assigned contacts, compare completed and interested calls, monitor total talk time, check conversion rates, assign customers, and inspect individual customer conversations.

<Info>
  Team Members is part of the **Sales Team** module. Access to this module and its customer information may depend on your workspace role and permissions.
</Info>

---

## Open Team Members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to Valuez AI">
    Sign in to the workspace containing your sales team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Sales Team">
    Select **Sales Team** from the main navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Team Members">
    Click **Team Members** to open the complete member list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also open this page by clicking **View Team Members** from Team Overview.

<Frame>
  ![Team Member](/images/team-member.png "Team Member")
</Frame>

---

## What You Can Do

The Team Members page helps authorized users:

- Find a team member by name or role
- Filter team information by date
- Review when a member joined
- Check the member's assigned contacts
- Review completed calls
- See how many contacts were interested
- Review total talk time
- Compare member conversion rates
- Sort the list by member or role
- Open an individual member's customer activity
- Assign customers to a team member
- Add a customer manually
- Upload customers using CSV or Excel
- Review customer call outcomes
- Open individual customer call details

---

## Understanding the Team Members Table

Each row represents one sales-team member.

| Column | What It Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Member** | The team member's name and the date they were added |
| **Role** | The role assigned to the team member |
| **Assigned Contacts** | Number of contacts assigned to the member |
| **Completed** | Number of completed calls |
| **Interested** | Number of interested leads associated with the member |
| **Total Talk Time** | Combined duration of the member's recorded calls |
| **Conversion Rate** | The member's returned lead-conversion percentage |
| **Actions** | Opens the member's detailed customer activity |

---

## Member

The **Member** column shows:

- Team member's name
- The date the person was added to the team

The date appears in the following format:

```text
since 10 Oct 2026
```

Use this information to distinguish new members from people with a longer performance history.

<Note>
  A recently added member may have fewer contacts, calls, and completed outcomes. Consider the member's start date when comparing performance.
</Note>

---

## Role

The **Role** column shows the member's assigned role.

Roles help explain the person's responsibilities within the sales operation. The exact roles available depend on how your workspace and team have been configured.

Use the role information when:

- Comparing people with similar responsibilities
- Searching for a specific type of team member
- Reviewing workload distribution
- Confirming that the correct person is handling assigned contacts

---

## Assigned Contacts

**Assigned Contacts** shows how many customer records have been assigned to the member.

An assigned contact is a customer or lead that the team member is responsible for handling.

The number can include contacts with outcomes such as:

- Qualified
- Callback
- No Outcome
- Pending

A high assigned-contact count does not necessarily mean that all contacts have already been called.

---

## Completed

**Completed** shows the number of assigned calling activities that reached a completed state.

Compare Completed with Assigned Contacts to identify whether work remains pending.

For example:

| Assigned Contacts | Completed | General Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 100 | 95 | Most assigned activity has been completed |
| 100 | 50 | A significant portion may still require attention |
| 20 | 0 | Assignments may be new, pending, or affected by another issue |

<Info>
  Completed does not automatically mean that the contact was interested or converted. Review Interested, Conversion Rate, and the underlying call outcomes.
</Info>

---

## Interested

**Interested** shows the number of leads identified as interested in the product, service, or offer.

Use this metric to understand:

- Which members are generating customer interest
- Whether the assigned contact list is suitable
- Whether additional follow-up is required
- Whether conversation quality needs to be reviewed

Before acting on an interested lead, open the member and review the customer's detailed call information.

---

## Total Talk Time

**Total Talk Time** shows the combined duration of the member's recorded calls.

Talk time can help you understand:

- Overall call engagement
- Whether conversations are progressing beyond the introduction
- Differences between members with similar assignments
- Whether call durations appear unusually short or long

Talk time should not be treated as a quality score. A short call may produce a valid result, while a long call may not produce a qualified lead.

---

## Conversion Rate

**Conversion Rate** displays the returned lead-conversion percentage for the member.

Use conversion rate together with:

- Assigned contacts
- Completed calls
- Interested leads
- Total talk time
- Customer outcomes
- Contact-list quality
- Time in the team

<Warning>
  Do not use conversion rate alone to evaluate a team member. A percentage based on a small number of calls may appear unusually high or low.
</Warning>

---

## Search for a Team Member

Use the search field in the page header to find a specific team member.

The search field displays prompts such as:

```text
Search by member name
```

```text
Find team member...
```

```text
Search by role...
```

As you enter text, the member list refreshes using the search value.

### Search tips

- Enter the member's complete name for a precise result.
- Enter part of the name if you are unsure of the spelling.
- Try the role name when looking for members with a particular responsibility.
- Remove the search text to return to the complete list.

If no matching member is found, the table displays:

```text
No team members found
```

---

## Filter Members by Date

Use the date-range control to limit team information to the selected period.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the date selector">
    Click the date-range control beside the search field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a start date">
    Select the beginning of the period you want to review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an end date">
    Select the end of the reporting period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the range">
    Apply the selection to refresh the member list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Clear the date selection from the date-range control to return to the unfiltered view.

<Tip>
  Use equivalent date ranges when comparing team performance. For example, compare one full week with another full week.
</Tip>

## Sort Team Members

The Team Members table supports sorting for:

- **Member**
- **Role**

Click a sortable column heading to change the order. Click it again to reverse the order.

Sorting is useful for:

- Arranging names alphabetically
- Grouping similar roles
- Finding a member in a large team
- Returning to the newest or oldest available ordering

The default ordering uses the member creation date, with the latest available records first.

---

## Navigate Between Pages

Use the page controls at the bottom of the table when the member list contains multiple pages.

Available rows-per-page options are:

- 10
- 15
- 25
- 50

Changing the number of rows returns the table to the first result page.

## Open a Team Member

Open a member using either of these methods:

- Click anywhere on the member's row
- Click the arrow button under **Actions**

The member-detail page shows the selected person's assigned customers and call activity.

## Member Summary Cards

The top of the member-detail page displays summary cards.

| Summary Card | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Assigned** | Total contacts assigned to the member |
| **Qualified** | Contacts classified as qualified |
| **Callback** | Contacts requiring or requesting another call |
| **Completed** | Calls that reached a completed state |
| **Pending** | Assigned contacts or calls that remain pending |

These values summarize the member's available assigned-customer activity.

<Note>
  Summary cards are calculated from the available member activity rather than only the rows visible on the current page.
</Note>

## Filter by Call Outcome

The outcome tabs let you focus on contacts with a particular result.

<Frame>
  ![Team Member](/images/team-member.png "Team Member")
</Frame>

Available tabs include:

| Tab | What It Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Every assigned customer returned for the member |
| **Qualified** | Contacts that met the applicable qualification criteria |
| **Callback** | Contacts requiring or requesting follow-up |
| **No Outcome** | Contacts without a final call outcome |
| **Pending** | Contacts whose calling activity remains pending |

Each tab displays the corresponding available count.

Selecting a tab refreshes the customer table and returns it to the first page.

## Search the Member's Customers

Use the search field on the member-detail page to find a customer.

You can search using:

- Contact name
- Telephone number

The field displays prompts such as:

```text
Search by contact
```

```text
Search phone number...
```

<Tip>
  If a telephone number does not return a result, try entering only the final digits. Stored telephone numbers may use different formatting.
</Tip>

---

## Filter Customer Activity by Date

Use the date-range control on the member-detail page to limit customer and call activity to a selected period.

The date filter works together with:

- Contact search
- Outcome tabs
- Sorting
- Pagination

For example, you can display:

- Qualified contacts from the current month
- Callback requests from the last seven days
- Pending calls within a selected period
- Activity for a particular contact and date range

## Assigned Customer Table

Each row represents a customer assigned to the selected team member.

| Column | What It Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Contact** | Customer name and telephone number |
| **Call Direction** | Direction of the recorded call |
| **Call Outcome** | Qualified, Callback, No Outcome, Pending, or another returned outcome |
| **Call Status** | Current processing or completion status |
| **Call Duration** | Length of the call |
| **Call Start Time** | Date and time when the call began |
| **Assigned On** | Date when the customer was assigned |
| **Actions** | Opens the customer's detailed call record |

---

## Contact

The Contact column shows:

- Customer name
- Customer telephone number

If a customer name is unavailable, the interface can display a placeholder while retaining the telephone number when available.

Treat telephone numbers and customer names as confidential information.

---

## Call Direction

**Call Direction** shows the direction associated with the call.

If no direction value is returned, the page uses:

```text
Outgoing
```

This generally indicates that the sales activity initiated the call to the assigned customer.

---

## Call Outcome

The customer table displays an outcome badge.

Supported displayed outcomes include:

| Outcome | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Qualified** | The customer met the applicable qualification requirements |
| **Callback** | A follow-up call is required or requested |
| **No Outcome** | No final outcome has been recorded |
| **Pending** | The contact remains pending |

If another outcome value is returned, the page can display that returned value.

---

## Call Status

**Call Status** displays the available processing or completion status for the customer's call.

The exact text depends on the call information returned to the workspace.

Use Call Status together with Call Outcome:

- Status explains the operational state of the call.
- Outcome explains the business result of the conversation.

These values can be different. For example, a call can be operationally completed but have no qualified business outcome.

---

## Call Duration

**Call Duration** shows how long the recorded call lasted.

Use duration to identify:

- Very short conversations
- Longer customer discussions
- Calls that may have ended early
- Records that may need transcript or recording review

A missing duration appears as a dash.

---

## Call Start Time

**Call Start Time** shows when the call began.

The date and time are displayed in a format such as:

```text
10 Oct 2026, 14:30
```

Use the timestamp to:

- Locate a customer interaction
- Match activity with another business record
- Investigate callback timing
- Confirm when the member contacted the customer

---

## Assigned On

**Assigned On** shows the date the customer was assigned to the team member.

Compare Assigned On with Call Start Time to understand how quickly the customer was contacted after assignment.

---

## Open Customer Call Details

Open a customer record by:

- Clicking the customer row
- Clicking the arrow in the Actions column

The customer details page can provide deeper information about the selected call, including available transcript, analysis, outcome, and timeline information.

<Info>
  The information available in customer details depends on whether the call was placed, connected, processed, and analyzed successfully.
</Info>

## Assign Customers

Click **Assign Customers** from the member-detail page to add and assign customers to the selected member.

The Assign Customers window supports two methods:

1. Add Manually
2. Upload File

Both methods add contacts to a shared customer pool. You must then select the required customers and click the final **Assign Customers** button to map them to the team member.

<Warning>
  Adding or uploading a customer does not automatically complete the assignment. After adding the records, select them from the customer table and confirm the assignment.
</Warning>

---

## Add a Customer Manually

Select the **Add Manually** tab to create an individual customer.

<Frame>
  ![Customer Manual](/images/customer-manual.png "Customer Manual")
</Frame>

### Manual customer fields

| Field | Required | Requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Customer Name** | No | If entered, must contain at least two characters |
| **Phone Number** | Yes | Valid 10-digit mobile number |
| **Alternate Phone Number** | No | Valid 10-digit number and different from the main number |
| **Email** | No | Must use a valid email format |
| **Address** | No | Maximum 120 characters |
| **Country** | No | Text field |
| **State** | No | Text field |
| **District** | No | Text field |
| **City** | No | Text field |
| **Pincode** | No | Six digits |
| **Source** | No | Origin such as Website or Referral |

### Phone-number requirements

The current manual-entry form accepts a 10-digit Indian mobile number beginning with:

- 6
- 7
- 8
- 9

Example:

```text
9876543210
```

<Note>
  Do not include spaces, hyphens, or a country code in the manual Phone Number field.
</Note>

### Add and assign the customer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Assign Customers">
    Open the assignment window from the selected team member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Add Manually">
    Open the manual customer form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the phone number">
    Enter the required valid 10-digit mobile number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add optional details">
    Enter the customer's name, email, address, location, pincode, or source when available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Customer">
    The customer is added to the available customer pool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the customer">
    Find the newly added customer in the table and select its checkbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the assignment">
    Click **Assign Customer** or **Assign Customers** to assign the selected record to the team member.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Upload Customers

Select the **Upload File** tab to import multiple customers.

<Frame>
  ![Upload Customer](/images/upload-customer.png "Upload Customer")
</Frame>

Accepted formats are:

```text
.csv
.xlsx
.xls
```

You can:

- Drag the file into the upload area
- Click the upload area and browse for a file

### Download the contact template

Click **Download Contact Template** to download the supported CSV header structure.

The template contains:

```csv
CustomerName,ContactNumber,AlternateContactNumber,Email,Address,City,District,State,Country,Pincode,Source
```

### Upload workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the template">
    Open Upload File and click **Download Contact Template**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter customer information">
    Add one customer per row without changing the column headings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the file">
    Save it as CSV or a supported Excel format.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the file">
    Drag the file into the upload area or browse for it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for processing">
    Valuez AI imports the contacts and refreshes the customer pool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select imported customers">
    Use the checkboxes to choose the customers to assign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the assignment">
    Click the assignment button at the bottom of the window.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Uploading a file imports the contacts but does not assign them automatically. Select the imported rows and confirm the assignment before closing the window.
</Warning>

---

## Customer Pool

The customer pool appears below both assignment methods.

It contains:

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Selection** | Checkbox used to select customers |
| **Name** | Customer name |
| **Phone Number** | Primary telephone number |
| **Email** | Customer email address |
| **City** | Customer city |
| **Status** | Whether the contact is available or already assigned |

Use the search field above the customer pool to find a contact by name.

Previously assigned contacts cannot be assigned again through the same selection process.

---

## Select Customers

Use the checkbox beside a customer to select the record.

You can also use the checkbox in the table header to select the available customers shown on the current page.

The assignment button indicates how many new customers are selected:

```text
Assign 1 Customer
```

or:

```text
Assign 5 Customers
```

The button remains unavailable until at least one new customer is selected.

## Customer Upload Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Start with the provided template">
    Download the contact template before preparing a bulk upload. Keeping the expected headings reduces import errors.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Use one customer per row">
    Do not place multiple telephone numbers or customers in the same spreadsheet row.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Keep phone numbers consistent">
    Use valid 10-digit mobile numbers without spaces, country codes, or punctuation when preparing Indian contact data.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Remove unnecessary duplicate records">
    Review the file before upload to reduce duplicate customer records and assignment confusion.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Assign contacts after uploading">
    Importing and assigning are separate actions. Select the uploaded customers and confirm the assignment.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Upload only authorized contacts">
    Ensure your organization has a lawful basis and appropriate consent to store and contact each customer.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Balance assignments">
    Review current assigned and pending totals before giving a member more customers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Prioritize callbacks">
    Callback requests are time-sensitive. Review them regularly and complete follow-up at the requested time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Verify qualified contacts">
    Open the customer call details before treating an automated qualification outcome as final.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Consider the member's start date">
    Recently added members may have limited historical activity and should not be compared directly with long-standing members without context.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Use outcomes and status together">
    Call Status describes the operational state, while Call Outcome describes the business result.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Protect customer information">

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No team members are displayed">
    Clear the search and date filter. Confirm that sales-team members exist in the current workspace and that you have permission to access the module.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Search does not find a member">
    Try entering only part of the person's name. Clear the date range and verify that you are using the correct workspace.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A member's metrics show zero">
    The member may not have assigned contacts or recorded calling activity for the available period. Open the member to verify their assignments.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="No customers are shown for a member">
    Clear the outcome tab, search, and date range. If the page remains empty, assign customers to the member.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Assign Customers button does not work">
    Confirm that the member was opened from the Team Members list and that your account has permission to assign customers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The customer cannot be added">
    Check that the main phone number is a valid 10-digit mobile number beginning with 6, 7, 8, or 9. Also check the optional email, alternate number, and pincode formats.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The alternate number is rejected">
    The alternate number must be valid and different from the main contact number.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The uploaded file is rejected">
    Confirm that the file is CSV, XLSX, or XLS. Download the contact template and verify that the expected column headings have not been changed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Uploaded customers are not assigned">
    Uploading imports the contacts into the customer pool. Select their checkboxes and click Assign Customers to complete the assignment.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A contact shows as already assigned">
    The contact is already mapped and does not need to be assigned again through the current workflow.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="No call details are available">
    The customer may not have been called, the call may not have connected, or processing may not have completed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Failed to load team members appears">
    Refresh the page and confirm your network connection. If the problem continues, contact your workspace administrator or Valuez AI support.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Privacy and Responsible Use

Team Members can contain employee-performance information and personal customer data.

Workspace administrators should ensure that:

- Access is limited to authorized users
- Customer information is collected lawfully
- Contact consent and communication preferences are respected
- Team performance information is handled confidentially
- Uploaded files are stored only in approved locations
- Duplicate local copies are deleted when no longer required
- Access is removed when a user's responsibilities change
- Applicable calling, privacy, employment, and data-retention rules are followed

<Warning>
  Your organization is responsible for complying with applicable rules governing customer consent, automated calling, sales communications, employee monitoring, and personal-data processing.
</Warning>
