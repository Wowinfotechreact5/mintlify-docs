Valuez AI is a multi-tenant voice and conversational automation platform. It combines configurable AI agents, business knowledge, inbound and outbound calling, campaign management, speech APIs, analytics, team permissions, billing, and operational monitoring.

This documentation follows the current Valuez frontend and backend source code. Features visible to you can vary according to your organization, subscription, platform entitlement, and assigned permissions.

## Platform areas

| Area | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Dashboard | Review performance, recent activity, active calls, agents, campaigns, and credit usage |
| AI Voice Agents | Create, configure, test, and monitor voice agents |
| Call Campaigns | Import contacts, configure outbound calling, launch campaigns, and inspect outcomes |
| Sales Team | Assign customers, operate the auto dialer, and review salesperson performance |
| APIs and Integrations | Manage API keys, telephony, website widgets, webhooks, and speech APIs |
| Settings and Billing | Manage workspace members, permissions, subscriptions, billing, credits, and profile settings |

## Recommended first-time workflow

1. Create or sign in to your account.
2. Confirm that the correct organization or workspace is selected.
3. Create an AI voice agent.
4. Configure its model, prompt, voice, transcriber, and language settings.
5. Create or connect a knowledge base.
6. Test known questions, unknown questions, interruptions, language changes, and escalation.
7. Assign a phone number or create an outbound campaign.
8. Monitor conversations, recordings, outcomes, credit usage, and errors.

## Access and permissions

Valuez applies multiple access controls. You must be authenticated, belong to the selected workspace, have an active subscription where required, and hold the necessary module permission. If a page is hidden or restricted, ask your workspace administrator to review your organization membership and permissions.
