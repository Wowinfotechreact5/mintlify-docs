The **Subscription & Plans** page helps you review your current credit usage, compare available Valuez AI plans, purchase a subscription, and add credits to an active plan.

Use this page to:

- Check how many credits your workspace has used
- Compare available subscription plans
- Review the features and limits included in each plan
- Select or change a subscription
- Enter billing and tax information
- Continue to the secure payment page
- Add credits to an active subscription
- Explore enterprise pricing

<Info>
  Plan prices, credits, limits, and feature availability can change. The values displayed in your Valuez AI dashboard at the time of purchase are the authoritative values for your workspace.
</Info>

## Credit Usage Summary

The top of the page displays the number of credits used during the current subscription period.

The usage summary includes:

| Information | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Credits used** | The number of credits already consumed |
| **Total credits** | The total credits currently allocated to the workspace |
| **Usage bar** | A visual representation of consumed credits |
| **Usage percentage** | The proportion of the available credit balance that has been used |

For example, a display such as `1,250 credits / 5,000 credits` means that 1,250 of the available 5,000 credits have been consumed.

<Note>
  Credits are usage units. The number of calls, messages, or speech operations available from a credit balance depends on the services used during each interaction.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Review your credit usage regularly, especially before launching a large outbound campaign or increasing the number of active agents.
</Tip>

## Understanding a Plan Card

Each plan is presented as a separate card.

| Card element | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Plan name** | Basic, Silver, Gold, or Platinum |
| **Description** | The intended usage level for the plan |
| **Credits per month** | The recurring credit allowance displayed for the plan |
| **Price** | The base plan price before applicable GST |
| **Start message** | Indicates whether the plan starts immediately or after the current plan expires |
| **Current Plan** | Identifies the plan already active for the workspace |
| **Most Popular** | Highlights the recommended plan in the current interface |
| **Get Started** | Opens the subscription review and billing form |
| **Schedule plan** | Indicates that the selected plan is intended to begin after the active plan expires |
| **View pricing** | Opens enterprise pricing information |
| **Read more** | Expands the plan card to show additional included features |
| **Read less** | Collapses the extended feature list |

## Current Plan

Your active plan is marked with the **Current Plan** button.

The current-plan button is disabled because the workspace is already subscribed to that plan.

The active plan determines limits such as:

- Available monthly credits
- Maximum AI agents
- API-key limits
- Campaign availability
- Contacts per campaign
- Concurrent campaign calls
- Team-member limits
- Virtual-number availability
- Reporting and analytics capabilities
- Support level

<Info>
  Some features may remain visible in the dashboard even when they are unavailable under your current plan. If you open a restricted feature, Valuez AI may ask you to upgrade.
</Info>

## Select a Subscription Plan

To purchase an available paid plan:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Compare the plan cards">
    Review the price, monthly credits, limits, and included capabilities for each plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expand the feature list">
    Click **Read more** if the complete list of included features is not already visible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the plan">
    Click **Get Started** on the plan you want to purchase.

    If the selected plan will begin after your current subscription ends, the button may display **Schedule plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the subscription">
    The **Review Your Subscription** window displays the selected plan, monthly credits, base amount, GST, total amount, and included limits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your billing details">
    Enter the required business, contact, and billing-address information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue to payment">
    Review the total and click **Continue to Payment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete payment">
    Valuez AI redirects you to the hosted payment page. Follow the payment provider’s instructions to complete the transaction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tabs>

<Warning>

## Billing Field Validation

Valuez AI validates billing information before continuing to payment.

| Field | Validation |
| --- | --- |
| **Legal / Company Name** | Cannot be empty |
| **Email Address** | Must use a valid email format |
| **Mobile Number** | Must contain 10 digits and begin with 6, 7, 8, or 9 |
| **Country** | Must be selected |
| **State** | Must be selected |
| **City** | Must be selected |
| **Postal Code** | Must contain 6 digits |
| **GSTIN** | If entered, must use a valid 15-character GSTIN format |
| **PAN / Tax ID** | Accepts up to 10 characters in the current form |

GSTIN and PAN/Tax ID values are automatically converted to uppercase in the form.

<Note>
  GSTIN, PAN/Tax ID, and Contact Person are optional in the visible checkout form. Other fields marked with an asterisk are required.
</Note>

## Taxes and Total Amount

The subscription review displays the price calculation before redirecting you to payment.

The current checkout calculation applies **18% GST** to the displayed base amount.

| Amount | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Base amount** | Subscription price before tax |
| **GST** | Tax calculated at the percentage displayed during checkout |
| **Total** | Base amount plus GST |

For example, if the base amount is ₹4,999:

- Base amount: ₹4,999.00
- GST at 18%: ₹899.82
- Total: ₹5,898.82

<Warning>
  Always confirm the final amount displayed in the Valuez AI checkout and hosted payment page before completing payment.
</Warning>

## Continue to Payment

After all required billing fields are valid, click **Continue to Payment**.

Valuez AI then requests a secure checkout link and redirects your browser to the hosted payment page.

<Info>
  The payment is completed outside the Subscription & Plans form. Follow the instructions shown by the hosted payment provider.
</Info>

Do not close or refresh the page while Valuez AI is preparing the payment redirect.

If a redirect does not occur, see the troubleshooting section below.

## Add Credits to an Active Plan

The **Credit top-up** section allows eligible workspaces to purchase additional credits without selecting another subscription plan.

The page displays:

- Your current available credit balance
- The conversion rate of ₹1 to 1 credit
- An **Add credits** button

<Info>

### Purchase additional credits

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Add credits">
    Click **Add credits** in the Credit top-up section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a top-up amount">
    Enter the amount you want to purchase or select one of the available preset amounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the credit calculation">
    The interface uses the displayed conversion of **₹1 = 1 credit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the billing information">
    Review or enter the required business, contact, and billing-address details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review taxes and total">
    Confirm the base amount, GST, total payment, and number of credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Proceed to payment">
    Click **Proceed to Pay** and complete the transaction on the hosted payment page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Estimate expected usage before selecting a plan">
    Estimate your monthly call volume, average call duration, campaign size, number of agents, required API keys, and team-member count.

    Select a plan that provides enough capacity for normal operations and expected peaks.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do not rely only on estimated call minutes">
    Estimated minutes are guidance, not a guaranteed allowance.

    Actual credit usage depends on the services used and the way each interaction is processed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Review feature limits before purchasing">
    Confirm that the selected plan supports the required number of agents, API keys, campaign contacts, concurrent calls, users, and phone numbers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Keep billing information accurate">
    Confirm the legal name, GSTIN, PAN or Tax ID, billing email, address, and postal code before payment.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Monitor credits during campaigns">
    Large campaigns can consume credits quickly. Review your balance before launching a campaign and while it is active.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Purchase top-up credits before the balance is exhausted">
    If you expect additional usage, add credits before the available balance becomes too low for normal operations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Contact Valuez AI for enterprise requirements">
    Use Platinum pricing for custom volumes, advanced routing, custom integrations, dedicated onboarding, or other enterprise requirements.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The Subscription & Plans page does not load">
    Refresh the page and check your internet connection.

    If the page still does not load:

    1. Sign out and sign in again.
    2. Confirm that you can access the active workspace.
    3. Try another supported browser.
    4. Contact Valuez AI support with a screenshot of the error.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My current plan is not marked correctly">
    Confirm that you are viewing the correct active workspace.

    Refresh the page to reload the latest subscription information. If the problem continues after a successful payment, contact support with the payment reference.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Get Started or Schedule plan does not respond">
    Wait for any existing loading operation to finish, then try again.

    Also check whether your browser blocks pop-ups, redirects, cookies, or scripts required by the checkout process.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My billing details do not load">
    Saved billing details may not exist for the workspace or may be temporarily unavailable.

    Enter the required billing information manually. Confirm that you are using the correct active organisation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My mobile number is rejected">
    Enter a 10-digit Indian mobile number beginning with 6, 7, 8, or 9.

    Do not include the country code, spaces, or punctuation in the field.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My postal code is rejected">
    Enter a six-digit Indian postal code using numbers only.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My GSTIN is rejected">
    Confirm that the GSTIN contains 15 valid characters and matches the required GSTIN structure.

    Remove spaces and punctuation. The form converts letters to uppercase automatically.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Continue to Payment is not working">
    Check every field marked with an asterisk and correct any validation errors.

    If the form is valid but no redirect occurs:

    1. Check your internet connection.
    2. Disable strict redirect blocking for the site.
    3. Try again in another supported browser.
    4. Do not submit repeatedly.
    5. Contact support if the payment page still does not open.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The payment page reports an error">
    Do not repeatedly submit the same payment without checking whether the first attempt was processed.

    Record the error message and payment reference, then review the Billing page or contact Valuez AI support.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Payment completed but the plan did not update">
    Wait briefly and refresh the dashboard.

    If the plan still does not update:

    1. Check the Billing page for the transaction.
    2. Sign out and sign in again.
    3. Confirm that the payment was made for the correct workspace.
    4. Contact support with the payment reference and purchase time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Add credits button is disabled">
    Credit top-ups require an active, unexpired subscription.

    Review your subscription status and expiry date. Purchase or renew a plan before trying to add credits.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My top-up amount is rejected">
    Enter a whole numeric amount between ₹100 and ₹10,00,000.

    Do not enter currency symbols, commas, letters, or unsupported characters in the amount field.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Credits were purchased but are not visible">
    Refresh the dashboard and reopen the Subscription & Plans page.

    If the balance still has not changed, check the payment status and contact support with the transaction reference.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I selected the wrong plan">
    Do not make another payment until you have confirmed the first transaction and the active or scheduled plan.

    Contact Valuez AI support with the workspace name, selected plan, payment reference, and purchase time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
