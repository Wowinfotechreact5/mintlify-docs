The **Knowledge Base** contains the business information your agent can use during customer conversations.

You can add:

- Business details
- Contact information
- Working hours
- Information from a public website page
- Services
- Products
- Frequently asked questions
- PDF documents

Clear and current knowledge helps the agent provide more useful and consistent answers.

<Warning>
  Always test important questions before using an agent with customers. If information is missing, outdated, or unclear, the agent may provide an incomplete or unsuitable answer.
</Warning>

---

## Agent Instructions and Knowledge

Agent Instructions and the Knowledge Base have different purposes.

| Area | Purpose | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Agent Instructions** | Defines how the agent should behave | “Ask one question at a time.” |
| **Knowledge Base** | Provides business information | “We are open from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.” |

Use **Agent Instructions** for:

- The agent's role
- Conversation steps
- Speaking style
- Questions to ask
- Restrictions
- Human-support behavior

Use the **Knowledge Base** for:

- Business information
- Products and services
- Prices
- Working hours
- Locations
- Policies
- Common questions
- Supporting documents

<Tip>
  Keep changing business information in the Knowledge Base. This makes prices, availability, working hours, and policies easier to update.
</Tip>

---

## Before You Begin

Collect approved information from the appropriate business teams.

Before adding anything, confirm that it is:

- Correct
- Current
- Approved for customers
- Easy to understand
- Consistent with other business information
- Safe to share
- Relevant to the agent's purpose

### Do Not Add

Never add:

- Passwords
- API keys
- Private tokens
- Payment-card information
- One-time passwords
- Private employee records
- Confidential internal documents
- Information customers are not allowed to receive
- Unapproved legal, financial, or medical claims

## Open the Knowledge Base

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Voice Agents">
    Sign in to Valuez AI and select **AI Voice Agents**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an agent">
    Open the agent you want to update.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the selected Knowledge Base">
    Open **Agent Settings → AI Model** and confirm that the correct Knowledge Base is selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Knowledge Base">
    Select the **Knowledge Base** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the sections">
    Add Business Information, Services, Products, FAQs, and Knowledge Documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the agent">
    Open **Test Agent** and ask questions about the information you added.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  An agent must have a selected Knowledge Base before it can be tested. If none is selected, Valuez AI displays **Knowledge Base Required**.
</Note>

## Website URL

Valuez AI can use the content from one saved public website page.

<Info>
  The website field reads the exact page you enter. It does not automatically read every page on your website.
</Info>

Good pages include:

- Product overview
- Service overview
- Pricing page
- Frequently asked questions
- Contact or branch information
- Customer-facing policy

Avoid:

- Login-protected pages
- Private company pages
- Temporary links
- Search-result pages
- Pages containing confidential information
- Pages containing mostly images
- Outdated pages

### Before Saving a Website

Confirm that:

- The link starts with `http://` or `https://`
- The page opens without signing in
- The information is current
- The information is approved for customers
- The page does not conflict with your FAQs or PDFs

The saved page is refreshed periodically. The interface can show:

- Refresh status
- Last successful refresh
- Next scheduled refresh
- Any refresh error

<Tip>
  If important information is spread across several pages, add it using Services, Products, FAQs, or focused PDF documents.
</Tip>

<Warning>

Confirm that the number:

- Is correct
- Is monitored
- Is approved for customer use
- Matches the support process described by the agent

## Working Hours

Set the opening and closing time for each day.

For a closed day, turn off that day's availability.

Example:

| Day | Status | Working hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Open | 09:00–18:00 |
| Tuesday | Open | 09:00–18:00 |
| Wednesday | Open | 09:00–18:00 |
| Thursday | Open | 09:00–18:00 |
| Friday | Open | 09:00–18:00 |
| Saturday | Open | 09:00–14:00 |
| Sunday | Closed | — |

Review the default schedule before saving it.

Also consider:

- Public holidays
- Temporary closures
- Lunch breaks
- Seasonal changes
- Different branch hours
- After-hours support

<Warning>
  The weekly schedule does not automatically explain public-holiday or temporary changes. Update the relevant information when your hours change.
</Warning>

## Service Description Example

```text
A 30-minute consultation for new business customers. It includes a
requirements discussion and a recommended next step. Final pricing depends
on the customer's requirements and is confirmed separately.
```

- What the service does

## Service Duration

Use a clear value such as:

```text
30 minutes
2 business days
Approximately 1 week
Depends on project scope
```

Do not provide an exact duration if it regularly varies.

---

## Service Pricing

Choose:

- **Fixed Price** when the same amount applies
- **Price Range** when the amount depends on the customer's requirements

Examples:

| Type | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed price | INR 1,500 |
| Price range | INR 1,500–3,000 |

If taxes, additional fees, or other conditions apply, include them in the description.

<Warning>
  Do not enter a price that could be understood as guaranteed when the final amount depends on an assessment, location, quantity, or customization.
</Warning>

---

## Appointment Requirement

Turn on **Appointment** when customers must book before receiving the service.

Make sure the Agent Instructions explain whether the agent:

- Collects an appointment request
- Can check availability
- Can confirm an appointment
- Must refer the customer to a team member

Do not tell customers that an appointment is confirmed unless it was actually completed.

## Write Natural Questions

Recommended:

```text
What time are you open on Saturday?
```

Less useful:

```text
Saturday operating schedule configuration
```

Add separate FAQs when different situations require different answers.

For example:

- What are your weekday hours?
- Are you open on Sunday?
- Are you open on public holidays?
- How can I contact support after working hours?

---

## Write Clear Answers

A good answer is:

- Correct
- Concise
- Customer-friendly
- Clear about conditions
- Consistent with other information
- Easy to understand when spoken aloud

Example:

```text
Our regular support hours are Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
India Standard Time. Public-holiday hours may vary.
```

Avoid answers such as:

```text
Yes.
```

A very short answer may not contain enough context when used during a conversation.

## FAQ Examples

| Question | Example answer |
| --- | --- |
| What are your working hours? | We are open Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST. |
| How can I contact support? | You can contact our support team using the email address or telephone number listed in our business information. |

<Warning>
  An FAQ helps guide the answer, but you should still test it. Do not assume that the agent will repeat every answer word for word.

- Working hours change
- A product or service changes
- Prices change
- Policies change
- The agent gives an incomplete answer
- Customers use unexpected wording
- Your support team identifies confusion

Delete obsolete FAQs after confirming that the information is no longer needed.

## Upload a PDF

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare the document">
    Remove outdated, private, or unnecessary information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Knowledge Documents">
    Select **Knowledge Documents** in the Knowledge Base.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose files">
    Drag PDF files into the upload area or select the area to browse.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review validation messages">
    Non-PDF files are rejected. Files larger than 10 MB are skipped.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the file list">
    Confirm the names and sizes of the selected documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Save & Next">
    Save the newly added files.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the upload">
    Return to the section and verify that the documents appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the information">
    Ask questions whose answers appear in each uploaded document.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you have not added a new file, the button may display **Next** instead of **Save & Next**.

---

## Prepare Useful PDFs

Use documents that are:

- Focused on one subject
- Clearly titled
- Divided into short sections
- Written with descriptive headings
- Current
- Easy to read
- Free from conflicting information
- Approved for customers

Avoid documents that contain:

- Poor-quality scans
- Handwritten information
- Important details shown only in an image
- Complex, unlabelled tables
- Several unrelated subjects
- Conflicting policy versions
- Password protection
- Confidential information
- Large amounts of irrelevant content

<Info>
  A successfully uploaded PDF may still contain layouts, tables, images, or scans that are difficult for the agent to use. Always test important information.
</Info>

---

## Use One Topic per Document

Recommended:

```text
Product-Catalog-2026-10.pdf
Pricing-Policy-2026-10.pdf
Cancellation-Policy-2026-10.pdf
Support-Guide-2026-10.pdf
Branch-Locations-2026-10.pdf
```

Avoid unclear names such as:

```text
final.pdf
latest2.pdf
document.pdf
new-final.pdf
```

Clear file names make documents easier to review and replace.

## Keep Information Consistent

Problems can occur when two sources contain different answers.

Examples:

- An FAQ says the office closes at 6:00 PM, but the website says 7:00 PM
- A product entry says INR 5,000, but a PDF says INR 4,500
- A product is marked unavailable, but an older document says it is available
- Two documents contain different refund periods

When you find conflicting information:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the correct information">
    Ask the team responsible for that business information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update every affected section">
    Correct Products, Services, FAQs, website content, and PDFs where necessary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove obsolete information">
    Delete or replace older documents and entries.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the question">
    Ask the agent the same question in several different ways.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Do not leave conflicting versions and expect the agent to know which one is correct.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

### Knowledge Base Required

The agent does not have a selected Knowledge Base.

To fix it:

1. Open **Agent Settings → AI Model**.
2. Select the correct Knowledge Base.
3. Save the change.
4. Return to **Knowledge Base**.
5. Add the required information.
6. Open **Test Agent**.

---

### Website Information Is Unavailable

Check that:

- The URL is complete
- The page opens without signing in
- The page is publicly accessible
- The page contains readable text
- The displayed refresh status does not show an error

Remember that Valuez AI uses the exact page entered, not the entire website.

---

### Website Information Is Outdated

1. Update the original website page.
2. Update Business Information, Products, Services, or FAQs if necessary.
3. Replace outdated PDFs.
4. Test the corrected answer.
5. Review the website refresh status.

---

### PDF Is Rejected

Confirm that:

- The file is a PDF
- The file is 10 MB or smaller
- The file is not damaged
- You can open it on your computer
- You have permission to update the Knowledge Base

---

### Some PDFs Were Skipped

Files larger than 10 MB are skipped.

When several files are selected, smaller valid PDFs may remain in the pending list. Review the warning and file count before saving.

---

### PDF Uploaded but the Agent Cannot Answer

Possible reasons include:

- The document contains poor-quality scanned pages
- Important information appears only in an image
- The layout is difficult to read
- A table has unclear labels
- Another source contains conflicting information
- The document is outdated
- The question uses very different wording

Try:

1. Creating a simpler PDF
2. Using clear headings
3. Adding the important answer as an FAQ
4. Removing conflicting documents
5. Testing several question variations

---

### Agent Gives an Old Price

Check every place where the price may appear:

- Product
- Service
- FAQ
- Website page
- Pricing PDF
- Policy document

Update every outdated source and test again.

---

### Agent Gives an Unsupported Answer

Check:

- Whether the correct information exists
- Whether another source conflicts with it
- Whether the correct Knowledge Base is selected
- Whether the customer was understood correctly
- Whether Agent Instructions explain what to do when information is missing

Add or correct an FAQ for important recurring questions.

---

### Changes Do Not Appear

1. Confirm that saving completed.
2. Check for an error notification.
3. Refresh the page.
4. Confirm the correct agent.
5. Confirm the correct Knowledge Base.
6. Reset the test conversation.
7. Start a new test session.

---

### Cannot Edit Knowledge

You may have permission to view the Knowledge Base but not change it.

Ask the workspace owner to review your access.

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Use the correct section">
    Add structured information such as services, products, prices, availability, contacts, and hours to their dedicated fields.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Use PDFs for detailed information">
    Detailed policies, guides, manuals, and procedures are better suited to focused PDF documents.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Avoid unnecessary duplication">
    Repeating the same fact in many places makes future updates more difficult and increases the chance of conflicting answers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Write for customers">
    Use clear language and remove internal abbreviations that customers may not understand.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Include important conditions">
    Explain conditions related to pricing, refunds, availability, eligibility, appointments, and delivery.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Test spoken answers">
    Listen to how the agent speaks addresses, numbers, email addresses, prices, product codes, and business names.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Review conversations">
    Use Conversation History to find unanswered questions and outdated information.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Keep approved originals">
    Keep your source documents in your normal business file-storage system. Do not use the Knowledge Base as the only copy.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
