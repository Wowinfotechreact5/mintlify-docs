The **My Profile** page is your personal account center in Valuez AI. It displays your identity, workspace membership, subscription information, account status, and security settings.

From this page, you can:

- Review your personal and workspace information
- Update your display name
- Upload and crop a profile photo
- Check your workspace role
- View your current subscription tier
- Confirm when your account was created or updated
- Change your account password

<Info>
  Some profile information is controlled by your account or workspace configuration and cannot be changed from this page.
</Info>

---

## Open My Profile

To open your profile:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the account menu">
    Sign in to the Valuez AI dashboard and open the account or profile menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select My Profile">
    Select **My Profile** from the available account options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your account">
    The page displays your personal details, workspace information, subscription tier, account status, and password controls.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Profile Overview

The profile overview at the top of the page provides a quick summary of your Valuez AI account.

Depending on your account and workspace configuration, the overview can include:

| Information | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile photo** | Your uploaded account image or automatically generated initials |
| **Full name** | The name associated with your Valuez AI account |
| **Email address** | The verified email address used by your account |
| **Organisation** | The workspace or organisation currently associated with your profile |
| **Account status** | Indicates whether the account is active or inactive |
| **Workspace role** | Your current role, such as Owner or Member |
| **Subscription plan** | The plan currently associated with the workspace |

If you have not uploaded a profile photo, Valuez AI displays initials generated from your name.

---

## Personal Details

The **Personal Details** card displays the personal information associated with your account.

| Field | Editable here? | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Full Name** | Yes | The display name shown across your Valuez AI account |
| **Email Address** | No | The verified email associated with your sign-in |
| **Workspace Role** | No | Your current level of access in the active workspace |
| **Account Created On** | No | The date your account was created |

### Verified email address

A **Verified** label is displayed next to your email address when it has been confirmed for your account.

Your email address cannot be changed from the My Profile page. If your organisation requires an email-address change, contact the appropriate workspace administrator or Valuez AI support.

<Warning>
  Do not create a second account simply to change your email address unless instructed to do so. A separate account may not automatically inherit your existing workspace access, assigned agents, or account history.
</Warning>

## Update Your Name

You can update the full name displayed on your Valuez AI account.

### Quick name editing

The profile header includes an edit icon next to your name.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the edit icon">
    Click the pencil icon next to your name in the profile header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your name">
    Replace the existing value with the name you want displayed.

    The name field accepts up to **40 characters**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the change">
    Click the checkmark icon to save the new name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can press **Escape** before saving to leave the quick-edit field.

### Edit from Personal Details

You can also select the edit icon next to **Full Name** in the Personal Details card.

This opens the **Update Profile** window, where you can review your name, email address, organisation, and profile picture.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/update-profile.png"
    alt="Valuez AI Update Profile window with full name, email address, organisation, and profile photo controls"
  />
</Frame>

To save your name from this window:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your full name">
    Update the value in the **Full Name** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the account information">
    Confirm that the displayed email address and organisation belong to the correct account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save Changes**.

    Valuez AI refreshes your profile information after the update is completed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The **Save Changes** button remains unavailable if the full-name field is empty.
</Note>

## Update Your Profile Photo

You can upload a profile photo using the camera button displayed beside your current avatar.

The profile interface accepts the following image types:

- JPG or JPEG
- PNG
- WebP

The upload interface recommends a maximum file size of **5 MB**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the camera button">
    Click the camera icon displayed next to your current profile photo or initials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an image">
    Select a JPG, PNG, or WebP image from your device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the crop">
    Move the image until the required area is inside the circular crop preview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the zoom">
    Use the zoom control to enlarge or reduce the image inside the crop area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the photo">
    Click **Save Photo** to upload the cropped image.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/crop-profile-photo.png"
    alt="Valuez AI Crop Profile Photo window with circular crop area and zoom control"
  />
</Frame>

After the update succeeds, the new profile photo appears in your profile and other supported areas of the dashboard.

### Choosing a suitable profile photo

For the best result:

- Use a square or nearly square image
- Place your face or main subject near the center
- Avoid placing important details close to the edges
- Use a clear image with sufficient lighting
- Preview the circular crop before saving
- Avoid extremely large image files

<Info>
  Valuez AI converts the cropped result into the profile image used by your account. Parts of the original image outside the selected crop area are not included.
</Info>

## Workspace and Organisation Information

The **Workspace & Organisation** card shows information about the organisation connected to your current profile.

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Organisation Name** | The name of your active Valuez AI workspace or organisation |
| **Subscription Tier** | The plan currently assigned to the workspace |
| **Status** | Indicates whether the associated account is active or inactive |
| **Last Updated** | The most recent available date for the profile record |

These fields are informational and cannot be changed directly from the My Profile page.

<Note>
  If your account belongs to multiple organisations, the information shown on this page is based on the currently active organisation.
</Note>

## Understanding Your Workspace Role

Your workspace role determines the level of access available to you.

The exact permissions available can depend on your organisation’s workspace configuration.

| Role | General meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | The account owns or controls the active workspace |
| **Member** | The account has been added to the workspace by another user |
| **Assigned role** | A workspace-specific role associated with the current organisation |

A role may affect which agents, calls, settings, billing information, or administrative functions you can access.

<Info>
  You cannot change your own workspace role from the My Profile page. Role and access changes are managed through workspace membership controls.
</Info>

For more information about member access, see [Workspace Members](/workspace-members).

---

## Subscription Information

Your current subscription tier appears in both the profile overview and the **Workspace & Organisation** card.

The displayed tier reflects the active workspace’s current plan information.

Your subscription tier can affect the availability or usage limits of platform features, including:

- AI agents
- Calling services
- Speech services
- API usage
- Workspace members
- Credits and usage allowances

To review available plans or manage your subscription, open [Subscription & Plans](/subscription-plans).

For payment and invoice information, see [Billing](/billing).

## Password and Security

The **Password & Security** section allows eligible users to update their account password.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/change-password.png"
    alt="Valuez AI Change Password window showing current password, new password, confirmation, and password strength"
  />
</Frame>

### Change your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the password window">
    Click **Change Password** in the Password & Security section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your current password">
    Enter the password currently associated with your Valuez AI account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a new password">
    Enter the new password you want to use.

    The new password must contain at least **8 characters**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the new password">
    Enter the same new password again in the **Confirm New Password** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review password strength">
    Check the displayed password-strength indicator before continuing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the password">
    Click **Save New Password**.

    The button becomes available only when the required information is valid.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Password field limits

The current implementation accepts up to **25 characters** in each password field.

| Requirement | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Current password** | Must be entered |
| **Minimum new-password length** | 8 characters |
| **Maximum field length** | 25 characters |
| **Password confirmation** | Must exactly match the new password |
| **Show or hide passwords** | Available through the visibility control |

<Warning>
  Your current password must be correct for the password update to succeed.
</Warning>

---

## Password Strength Indicator

As you enter a new password, Valuez AI calculates a password-strength score using the following characteristics:

- At least 8 characters
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one lowercase letter
- At least one number
- At least one special character

The displayed strength can be:

| Rating | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Weak** | The password meets few of the recommended characteristics |
| **Medium** | The password includes several different character types |
| **Strong** | The password uses a broader combination of character types |

Only the minimum length and matching confirmation are required by the visible form before submission. However, using all listed character types provides a stronger password.

### Example password structure

A strong password can combine:

- Uppercase letters
- Lowercase letters
- Numbers
- Symbols
- A length greater than the minimum requirement

<Warning>
  Do not copy a password example from documentation or share your password with another person. Create a unique password that is not used for another service.
</Warning>

---

## Show or Hide Passwords

The Change Password window includes a **Show passwords** control.

When selected, it displays the content of all three password fields:

- Current Password
- New Password
- Confirm New Password

Select **Hide passwords** to mask the values again.

<Tip>
  Keep passwords hidden when using Valuez AI in a shared, public, or recorded environment.
</Tip>

## Accounts That Use Google Sign-In

If you normally access Valuez AI with **Sign in with Google**, your sign-in identity may be managed through your Google account.

The availability and outcome of password changes can depend on how your Valuez AI account was originally created.

If you cannot update a password because you use Google Sign-In:

1. Continue using **Sign in with Google** on the login screen.
2. Confirm that you are signed in to the correct Google account.
3. Manage your Google password through your Google Account security settings.
4. Contact Valuez AI support if you need help determining your account’s sign-in method.

<Note>
  Changing your Google Account password is separate from updating a password stored directly by Valuez AI.
</Note>

## Account Status

The profile header displays your current account status.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | The account is currently enabled |
| **Inactive** | The profile record is not currently marked as active |

If your account unexpectedly appears as inactive, contact your workspace owner or Valuez AI support.

An inactive account may not be able to access all workspace functions.

## Profile Changes and Other Dashboard Areas

After a successful profile update, Valuez AI refreshes the stored profile details.

Changes to your name or profile image may also appear in other supported dashboard areas, such as:

- Account menus
- Profile headers
- Workspace activity
- Administrative user information

If the previous information remains visible, refresh the page and sign in again if necessary.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My profile details do not load">
    Refresh the page and try again.

    If Valuez AI displays an error banner, click **Try again** to reload the profile.

    If the problem continues:

    1. Confirm that your internet connection is working.
    2. Sign out and sign in again.
    3. Confirm that you still have access to the workspace.
    4. Try another supported browser.
    5. Contact Valuez AI support with a screenshot of the error.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot save my name">
    Make sure the Full Name field is not empty.

    The field supports a maximum of 40 characters. Remove unnecessary characters if the entered name is too long.

    If the button remains unavailable, close the editor, reopen it, and try again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My updated name is not visible everywhere">
    The dashboard refreshes stored profile information after a successful update. If an old name is still displayed:

    1. Refresh the page.
    2. Navigate to another page and return.
    3. Sign out and sign in again.
    4. Clear the browser cache if the old value continues to appear.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My image format is not accepted">
    Confirm that the file is a valid JPG, JPEG, PNG, or WebP image.

    Changing only the filename extension does not convert an image. Open the file in an image editor and export it using a supported format.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The image cannot be read">
    The file may be damaged, incomplete, or stored in an unsupported format.

    Try opening the image on your device. If it opens correctly, export or save a new copy and upload that copy.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The photo crop is incorrect">
    Reopen the image selector and upload the photo again.

    Use the crop controls to reposition the image, and adjust the zoom until the required area appears inside the circular preview.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My profile photo does not update">
    Confirm that you clicked **Save Photo** after adjusting the crop.

    Then:

    1. Wait for the save operation to finish.
    2. Refresh the page.
    3. Try a smaller image.
    4. Try another supported image type.
    5. Upload a newly exported copy of the image.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The new passwords do not match">
    The **New Password** and **Confirm New Password** fields must contain exactly the same value.

    Passwords are case-sensitive. Check uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, symbols, and accidental spaces.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Save New Password is disabled">
    The password button remains unavailable until:

    - The current-password field is filled
    - The new password contains at least 8 characters
    - The confirmation matches the new password
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My current password is rejected">
    Confirm that you entered the password for the correct Valuez AI account.

    If you normally sign in with Google, continue using Google Sign-In. If you use email and password but cannot remember the password, use the password-recovery option from the login page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot change my email address">
    The email field is read-only on the My Profile page because it is linked to your account identity.

    Contact your workspace administrator or Valuez AI support if the account email needs to be changed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My organisation or role is incorrect">
    First confirm that you are viewing the correct active workspace.

    Organisation and role information cannot be edited from My Profile. Contact the workspace owner or an authorised administrator to review your membership.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My subscription tier is incorrect">
    Open **Subscription & Plans** or **Billing** and confirm the active workspace’s subscription.

    If the displayed plan still appears incorrect, contact Valuez AI support and include the workspace name shown on your profile.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Security Best Practices

To help protect your Valuez AI account:

- Use a unique password that is not used elsewhere
- Use a mixture of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols
- Do not send passwords through email, chat, or support screenshots
- Keep passwords hidden when other people can see your screen
- Sign out when using a shared device
- Review your workspace and role information regularly
- Report unexpected profile or organisation changes immediately
- Keep access to your associated email or Google account secure

<Warning>
  Valuez AI staff should never need your complete password. Do not share it with support personnel or another workspace member.
</Warning>

---

## Before Contacting Support

If you need assistance with your profile, collect the following information:

- The email address associated with your account
- The organisation name shown on the profile
- The affected profile field
- A description of the action you attempted
- The approximate time the issue occurred
- A screenshot of the error message

Remove or hide passwords and other sensitive information before sharing a screenshot.
