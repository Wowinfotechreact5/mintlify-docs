Valuez AI is a voice and conversational automation platform for creating, operating, and monitoring AI agents.

The platform combines AI agents, business knowledge, voice calling, outbound campaigns, speech services, integrations, analytics, workspace management, billing, and usage monitoring.

This user guide explains how to configure and operate the capabilities available in your Valuez AI workspace.

<Info>
  The features available to you may depend on your workspace, assigned permissions, subscription plan, and enabled integrations.
</Info>

## How Valuez AI Works

A typical Valuez AI workflow includes the following stages:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare your business information">
    Collect the information that the AI agent needs to answer customer questions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an AI agent">
    Define the agent's name, purpose, language, voice, and behavior.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Train the agent">
    Add approved business knowledge, frequently asked questions, and operating instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the agent">
    Verify responses and conversation behavior before connecting the agent to customers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a communication channel">
    Connect the agent to an available phone, website, or API integration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run a controlled test">
    Perform a limited test before enabling production calls or a large campaign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor activity">
    Review conversations, call logs, analytics, outcomes, errors, and credit usage.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recommended First-Time Setup

For your first Valuez AI implementation:

1. Sign in to the correct workspace.
2. Review the Dashboard Overview.
3. Create one AI agent for a clearly defined purpose.
4. Add accurate business knowledge.
5. Test common customer questions.
6. Correct any inaccurate or unclear responses.
7. Connect one supported communication channel.
8. Perform a controlled test.
9. Review the resulting conversation or call log.
10. Monitor credit usage.
11. Expand the workflow only after successful testing.

<Tip>
  Start with one focused use case. A clearly defined customer-support, lead-qualification, or appointment workflow is easier to test and improve than a single agent expected to handle every business process.
</Tip>

## Before You Begin

Prepare the following information before creating an agent:

- Organisation name
- Agent purpose
- Intended audience
- Supported customer workflow
- Preferred language
- Preferred voice
- Business description
- Frequently asked questions
- Product or service information
- Operating hours
- Escalation instructions
- Approved phone numbers
- Campaign contacts, when applicable
- Required integrations

## Access and Permissions

The pages and actions available to you can vary according to:

- Active workspace
- Workspace role
- Assigned agent access
- Subscription plan
- Account status
- Enabled platform features
- Available integrations

If a documented feature is not visible:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the active workspace">
    Confirm that you selected the correct organisation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your access">
    Ask the workspace owner to confirm your role and assigned access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the subscription">
    Confirm that the feature is included in the active plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review integrations">
    Confirm that any required integration has been configured.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report the problem">
    Use Report a Problem if the feature should be available but remains inaccessible.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Security Reminder

Never include the following in screenshots, problem reports, or messages:

- Passwords
- API keys
- Access tokens
- OTP codes
- Payment credentials
- Private customer information
- Confidential conversations
- Sensitive knowledge-base content

<Warning>
  If a password, API key, token, or other secret is accidentally exposed, revoke or rotate it immediately.
</Warning>
